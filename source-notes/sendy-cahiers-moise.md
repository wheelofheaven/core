## Source type and purpose

Speculative essay; Sendy's first statement of the reading of Genesis as colonisation by "Théosites". Scientific and historical assertions require independent appraisal.

## Reported content and locators

Satan") as the serpent; sons of the Elohim taking human wives; Babel as « la tour de lancement d'un astronef prématuré »; atomic Sodom; a nuclear Flood with an ark carrying documents.
