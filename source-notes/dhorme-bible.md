# La Bible — Ancien Testament, Bibliothèque de la Pléiade

## Source identity and access

Wheel source ID `dhorme-bible`. *La Bible — Ancien Testament I*, édition
publiée sous la direction d'Édouard Dhorme; introduction par Édouard
Dhorme; traductions et notes par Édouard Dhorme, Franck Michaéli et
Antoine Guillaumont. Bibliothèque de la Pléiade, © Éditions Gallimard,
1956. Access: a publisher's digital extract (Electre) comprising the
title pages, the measures table, and Genesis 1–3 with the translation and
Dhorme's apparatus, inspected 2026-10-24. The full volume has not been
inspected; access for anything beyond the extract remains metadata-level.

## Source type and purpose

Scholarly French translation of the Hebrew Bible in the rationalist
philological tradition: Élohim preserved as a proper plural, Iahvé for
the Tetragrammaton, literal operational register, apparatus notes keyed
to verses. The canon's biblical quotations descend from this translation
(woh-claim-0066).

## Reported content

At the inspected extract:

- Genesis 1:20: «Que les eaux foisonnent d'une foison d'animaux vivants
  et que des volatiles volent au-dessus de la terre, à la surface du
  firmament des cieux!» (note *des* volatiles — the project digitization
  of TBWTT 2:19 reads *les* volatiles, a one-word variance)
- Genesis 1:26: «Faisons l'homme à notre image, à notre ressemblance!
  Qu'ils aient autorité…» (TBWTT 2:26 reads «Qu'il ait autorité», the
  verb moved to the singular)
- Genesis 1:21: «Élohim créa donc les grands dragons et les animaux
  vivants qui remuent, ceux dont les eaux foisonnent, selon leur espèce,
  et tout volatile ailé, selon son espèce. Élohim vit que c'était bien.»
- Note on vv. 20–23: «Cinquième jour. Nouvelle séparation : animaux
  aquatiques et oiseaux des cieux. Les dragons (verset 21), hébreu
  *tanninim*, monstres marins (*Isaïe*, XXVII, 1; *Psaumes*, LXXIV, 13;
  *Job*, VII, 12) de la nature des serpents (*Exode*, VII, 9-12;
  *Deutéronome*, XXXII, 33).»
- Genesis 1:24: «Que la terre fasse sortir des animaux vivants selon leur
  espèce : bestiaux, reptiles, bêtes sauvages, selon leur espèce!»
- Note on Genesis 3:1: the *nâhâsh* is «le nom générique du serpent» —
  the antagonist «…qu'on identifiera à Léviathan d'après *Isaïe*, XXVII,
  1…» (the clause before this span, naming the antagonist to be
  vanquished per *Job* 26:13, could not be resolved word-for-word at the
  extract's scan quality; two independent reads differ on one word, so
  only the agreed span is quoted here)

## Basis or method

Dhorme's own translation from the Masoretic text with philological
apparatus; the dragon rendering is derived in the apparatus from the
Hebrew *tanninim* with inner-biblical cross-references.

## Limitations and criticism

The extract is partial (Genesis 1–3 and front matter only) and digital;
page numbers of the printed volume were not visible in the extract. The
1956 first printing and later Pléiade printings have not been collated.
Dhorme's renderings are one scholarly tradition's choices; his philology
carries no brief for any framework reading.
