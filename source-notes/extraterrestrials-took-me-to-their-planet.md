# Les extra-terrestres m'ont emmené sur leur planète (second Message)

## Source identity and access

Stable Wheel source ID: `extraterrestrials-took-me-to-their-planet`.
French text in the project digitization (`ETTMTTP-c:p` locators); first printing not collated.
Access: passages listed below.

## Source type and purpose

Second Raëlian Message (1975/76): Raël's autobiographical account of his life before 1973, the publication of the first book, and the report of the 7 October 1975 contact. Canon text; biographical content is self-report.

## Reported content and locators

- ETTMTTP-1:7–61: childhood, Catholic boarding schools, leaving school at fifteen, singing career, accident, Dijon venture, founding a motor-sport magazine in Clermont-Ferrand.
- ETTMTTP-1:63–70: notes put in order in the first days after 13 December 1973; manuscript sent to a Paris publisher; *Le Grand Échiquier* appearance (date not verified); manuscript recovered; the book « à l'automne 1974 … sortait des presses ».
- ETTMTTP-2:95: the president of the Council of the Eternals referred to as Iahvé.
- ETTMTTP-3:172: 1946 as year one.
