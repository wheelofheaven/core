## Reported content and locators

Introduction; sections 12–15, especially printed pp. 18 and 20–21; bibliography.

Bigliardi reconstructs Sendy’s broader esoteric programme and identifies his apparent allusion to Raël. He reports a March 1974 Clermont-Ferrand lecture from a newspaper source. The newspaper was not inspected here, nor does this show Vorilhon attended. His dating of the accusation to 1974 needs checking against the supplied French chapter’s 1975 references.
