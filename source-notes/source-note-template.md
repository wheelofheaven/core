# [Source title]

## Source identity and access

Record the Wheel source ID, exact edition or representation, access route, and
whether full text, project digitization, excerpt, abstract, metadata, or a
secondary citation was inspected.

## Source type and purpose

Identify genre, authorship, date, intended purpose, and relevant textual or
methodological context.

## Reported content

Record what the source itself reports or argues. Use precise locators. Do not
convert the source report into historical occurrence.

## Basis or method

Record textual witnesses, translation, data, analysis, or argumentative basis
where known. Do not fill missing detail by assumption.

## Limitations and criticism

Distinguish limitations acknowledged by the source, external criticism, access
limits, textual uncertainty, and the reviewer's concerns.

## Wheel interpretation

Label the inferential step. Explain which claim the source informs, how Wheel
uses it, and what it does not establish.

## Related sources and dependencies

Identify reused texts, editions, translations, later interpretations,
criticisms, and shared assumptions.
