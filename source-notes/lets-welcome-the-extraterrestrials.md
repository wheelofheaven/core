# Accueillir les Extra-Terrestres (third book)

## Source identity and access

Stable Wheel source ID: `lets-welcome-the-extraterrestrials`. English
title in the registry: *Let's Welcome The Extraterrestrials*.

The text inspected is the French text, with its English column, in the
project digitization (`data-library/lets-welcome-the-extraterrestrials/`;
`LWTE-c:p` locators). The digitization metadata gives 1979, four
chapters and 836 paragraphs. The first printing has not been collated.
Access is limited to the passages listed under "Verification log".

## Source type and purpose

This is the third Raëlian book (1979). Its author is Raël, and it is
treated as canon. Unlike the two dictated messages, it is not a report of
a contact. It is a compilation in several voices:

| Chapter (French title, per digitization) | Voice structure, as inspected |
| --- | --- |
| 1 *Questions Revenant le Plus Souvent* | Readers' questions (marked "Question:") answered by Raël in his own voice |
| 2 *Nouvelles Révélations* | Raël's presentation of further revelations. Internal voice structure not inspected for this note |
| 3 *Une Religion Athée* | A continuous essay in Raël's own voice, signed "RAEL" with the movement's Geneva address (`LWTE-3:88`) |
| 4 *Commentaires et Témoignages de Raëliens* | Signed contributions by Raëlians, not by Raël. Marcel Terrusse's essay *Le Raëlisme sous l'œil de la science* runs `LWTE-4:1–79` in three sections (I `4:1–25`; II, the new history of humanity, `4:26–58`, whose continental-breakup passage `4:26–46` is the one `woh-claim-0071` cites; III, the Raëlian baptism, `4:59–79`); a second Terrusse piece begins at `4:95` (the gene-synthesis testimony at `4:129`, cited by `woh-claim-0074`). Inspected 2026-11-11 in the data-library chapter file |

**Voice rule (founder direction, 2026-11-08):** Raël's own commentary
voice in this book is canon. Every use must be labelled as Raël's
commentary voice, distinct from the dictated messages
(`the-book-which-tells-the-truth`,
`extraterrestrials-took-me-to-their-planet`). The weight of chapter 4's
signed contributions by other authors stays open; `woh-claim-0071`
carries that question.

## Reported content and locators

- `LWTE-1:75`: a reader's question that presupposes that Moses, Jesus,
  Buddha, Mohammed "et tous les grands prophètes qui vivent sur la planète
  des Éternels" will accompany the Elohim to the embassy. The premise is
  the questioner's; Raël's answer (`LWTE-1:76–78`) does not dispute it.
- `LWTE-1:87` (Raël): the priority is to build the embassy and to welcome
  the creators there "en compagnie des anciens messagers, Moïse, Jésus,
  Mahomet, Bouddha, etc."
- `LWTE-3:27` (Raël, essay voice): the Raëlian "reconnaît Jésus comme étant
  l'envoyé de nos créateurs, tout comme Moïse, Bouddha, Mahomet, Joseph
  Smith et tous les grands prophètes ayant existé, et il attend leur retour
  en compagnie des Elohim". This is the only place in the three canon books
  where Joseph Smith is named.
- `LWTE-3:82` (Raël, essay voice): the embassy "dans laquelle ils viendront
  avec leurs anciens messagers, Moïse, Jésus, Bouddha, Mahomet et quelques
  autres comme les Ecritures l'annonçaient".

## Basis or method

The book is self-authored by the canon's reporter: answers, commentary
and an essay. Its claims about the prophets restate or extend the
dictated messages in Raël's own voice. They report no new contact.

## Limitations and criticism

- The English column is an edition translation. At `LWTE-3:27` it renders
  the French faithfully on the points used here (Smith is named). The
  French and English columns are reported misaligned in chapter 4 (see
  `woh-claim-0071`'s review); that was not checked here.
- Not checked: whether later combined editions keep the `LWTE-3:27` list
  unchanged.
- External criticism of the movement's prophetology is registered in the
  records that use this note (for example `aliens-adored`, metadata only).

## Wheel interpretation

`woh-claim-0038` (0.1.1) and `woh-claim-0077` use this book for the named
lists and for the return clause. Each use is labelled as Raël's commentary
voice. The book places no figure at the table scene of
`ETTMTTP-2:62–64`. In particular, it does not seat Joseph Smith there,
and it assigns no ordinal position to any figure.
