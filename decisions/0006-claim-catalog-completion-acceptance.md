# ADR 0006: Acceptance of the completed claim catalog (0054-0069)

## Context

After ADR 0005 accepted claims 0005-0053, four further distillation
batches (7 through 10, 2026-08-16) closed the corpus survey: the
instrument and posture records (0054-0058), the coverage-completion
records (0059-0061), the episode and method records (0062-0065), and
the provenance and context records (0066-0069). Each batch was drafted
with controlling specifications and scoped evidence maps, presented at
a founder gate, and its 41 derivative pages were bound across the
datasets, library, timeline, and wiki surfaces before this ruling. The
founder directed promotion of the full range.

## Decision

Claims **woh-claim-0054 through woh-claim-0069** are promoted from
`draft` to `accepted` lifecycle. As in ADR 0005: acceptance means
these records are the current project specifications of what the
framework claims — not findings, not validations, not evidence
upgrades.

Explicitly ruled with the acceptance:

- **The 0064 relation is settled at `contextual`.** The neo-euhemerist
  method record was drafted `contextual` with the question flagged
  (the corpus's own entry calls it a foundational method). Acceptance
  at `contextual` is the ruling; the entry's phrasing remains
  descriptive, and elevation would be a new founder ruling under the
  `woh-claim-0023` precedent, kept named in the record's revision
  triggers.
- **The consonance disciplines are ratified as a family**: the named
  forbidden moves — counting rows as evidence (0060), feasibility as
  occurrence (0065), taxonomic slot as verdict (0067), Dead Sea
  geology as strike evidence (0062), sites or alignments as chronology
  support (0058) — are the project's position, and each is a standing
  revision trigger on its record.
- **The no-enlistment rule is ratified corpus-wide**: scientists,
  translators, journalists, and declined precursor authors are
  registered at their own grades and enlisted for nothing (0057, 0061,
  0065, 0066, 0067, 0068).
- **The join records carry their parents' risks undiluted**: 0059
  (creation week onto the age-lattice) inherits 0003 and 0039
  explicitly, and the timeline chapters — not the record — carry the
  per-age assignments.

Pre-promotion consistency sweep: **0064 gained `contested`**, aligning
the last framework-labeled record with the catalog's convention that
externally unendorsed labels carry the contested flag (the ADR 0005
sweep's rule, previously applied to 0006 and 0041).

Still not promoted: claims **0001-0004** (the founding pilots and the
reverse pilot), which remain `draft` pending their own review.

Unchanged: every record's version, public label, scope, alternatives,
and revision triggers (0064's first trigger reworded only to record
this ruling). Evidence maps deliberately remain `draft` — they track
inspection state, not doctrine.

## Consequences

### Enables

- The catalog is complete at survey level and fully accepted above the
  pilots: 65 accepted records, 4 draft pilots. New artifacts ground
  against accepted records across every section, and the founder pause
  now prices only genuinely new claims.
- The 41 newly bound pages (datasets, library index, timeline ages,
  wiki clusters) stand on ratified records.

### Costs and risks

- As in ADR 0005: accepted status can misread as validation; the
  "lifecycle is not truth" rule and each record's evidence status are
  the standing answer.
- The consonance disciplines are only as strong as their enforcement
  in prose; the named triggers are the mechanism.

## Open items carried out of the ruling

- The `pangaea.md` inconsistency (continent-raising dated to Scorpio
  against the timeline chapters' Sagittarius placement) awaits an
  editorial correction; the timeline chapters control per 0059.
- Pilots 0001-0004 await their own review.

## Validation and review

The catalog validates end to end (69 records; specifications synced;
publication integration checked). Review this ADR under the same
condition as ADR 0005: accepted records accumulating trigger
conditions without revision.
