# ADR 0014: Founder rulings from the 2026-11 canon-consistency scan; revisions to 0036 and 0071

## Context

On 2026-11-09 a read-only scan checked the English site against every
accepted record's exclusions and ADR 0013: 201 findings on 86 pages.
Factual and record-exclusion fixes were made on 2026-11-10 in English
and carried into the nine translations. Seven items could not be fixed
without changing a page's thesis or a record, and were put to the
founder.

## Decision

The founder directed, on 2026-11-10:

1. **Design premise and detection (`woh-claim-0036` → 0.2.0).** The
   premise that all terrestrial life was designed and engineered by the
   Elohim is canon (`woh-claim-0001`) and is stated directly. The record
   now includes reporting, in their own voice and with the replies, the
   scientists who have argued that life may have been deliberately seeded
   (Crick and Orgel 1973; Crick 1981) or that an unguided origin is
   mathematically improbable, usually with panspermia (Hoyle and
   Wickramasinghe 1978). The founder's direction was the broader
   formulation "signs of artificiality or a mathematical unlikelihood";
   review narrowed the attribution to what each work argues. It still excludes presenting detection as
   established science or consensus, and the ID movement's designer. The
   honesty clause becomes: mainstream biology does not accept that design
   has been detected; a minority of scientists have argued for deliberate
   seeding or for the improbability of an unguided origin, and the
   corpus reports them as such. *The Signature and the
   Designers* and *Age of Scorpio* are aligned to this.
2. **Raël's commentary on dating and drift (`woh-claim-0071` → 0.3.1).**
   LWTE-1:28–31 (non-constant atomic behaviour; continents moving apart
   by several hundred kilometres a year at the beginning) is registered
   as the canon's commentary layer, labelled as Raël's commentary. It is
   not adopted as a reconciling mechanism.
3. **The comparative tier scale** in *The Forty Chairs* and the
   prophets list is kept as the corpus's own device for organising
   comparison, labelled as such: not a canon ranking, not an authenticity
   verdict. Joseph Smith is not graded "Canonical" (`woh-claim-0077`,
   `woh-claim-0079`).
4. **The PURSUE dispatches and *Age of Aquarius* §III/§IX** read current
   events as context, not signs: no "disclosure curve", no dated window,
   no UAP or post-war cluster as evidence or as movement toward the
   return (`woh-claim-0057`, `0015`, `0031`).
5. **The embassy page's "convergence" section** is recast as
   resemblance; the name Maitreya is credited to Raël's post-canon
   writings (`woh-claim-0015`, `0078`).
6. ***Age of Taurus* §X.4–5 (Giza):** the redating theses are registered
   at their own grade, not as evidence (`woh-claim-0058`); the promised
   dedicated Giza chapter is withdrawn.
7. **The 14 March 1978 message** quoted by the cosmic-competition entry,
   which is not in the three canonical books and whose publication could
   not be traced, is removed with the section built on it.

Also directed on 2026-11-10: the Sendy pages do not assume that the
Message depends on Sendy, and do not argue independence from the
differences (`woh-claim-0072` unchanged).

## Consequences

- Records 0036 (0.2.0) and 0071 (0.3.1) are revised; bound pages bump
  their `core_versions`.
- The page changes above are made in English and carried into the nine
  languages with translator and reviewer per language.

## Validation and review

The catalog validates with publication integration checked after the
revisions. Review this ADR if a discriminating biological test between
designed and evolved life is proposed (0036's trigger), or if the source
of the 1978 message is located.
