# The life-engineering convergence

## Exact proposition

Present-day humanity's acquisition of life-engineering capability —
genetic engineering, synthetic biology, synthetic genomics, with
pantropy and terraforming as projected extensions — read as the
framework's most concrete predicted convergence: the canon describes
the creation in operational engineering vocabulary and states that
its creations will do the same work in time, and design-build-test
biology arriving within one long lifetime of the canon's dictation is
read as that trajectory tracking. The yield is mechanism-availability
and nothing more.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `inferred`.

## The grade discipline

The record's named forbidden move is the slide from feasibility to
occurrence: that life can now be engineered says nothing about
whether it was. The scientific milestones (the 2010 synthesized-genome
bacterial cell; the minimal-genome consolidation) are carried at
documentary strength as what mainstream science has done, the
disciplines remain mainstream enterprises enlisted for nothing, and
the science entries keep their own documentary grade. The canonical
side rides on `woh-claim-0030`'s created-creators cycle — which is
what makes this record derived rather than a free-standing science
claim. Both deflations are carried in full: authorship-era projection
(1970s engineering vocabulary read back as foresight) and
feasibility-is-cheap (once any technology can mimic a mythic act,
every such myth becomes 'feasible').
