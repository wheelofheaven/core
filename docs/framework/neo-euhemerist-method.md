# The neo-euhemerist method

## Exact proposition

The corpus's working method: myth's divine beings as garbled memory
of real historical figures, extending Euhemerus's gods-as-kings by
substituting a technologically advanced civilization for the kings —
operationalized by Sendy's five reader-conditions (what the reader
must have) and Biglino's three-step procedure (what the reader must
do), and extended beyond both by the precessional architecture and
the ongoing-alliance frame. The Elohim-civilization hypothesis
(`woh-claim-0001`) is the method's output, not its premise.

An `interpretation`, `contextual`, `framework`.

## The equipment, the procedure, the honesty

Sendy's hologram passage supplies the equipment — the text,
astronautics, the sciences, refusal of explanation by the
inexplicable, an intelligible universe — with the fourth condition as
the method's philosophical spine. Biglino supplies the procedure:
original language, tradition set aside, the literal reading. The
record claims no academic standing for any of it: mainstream
scholarship engages the position as the paleocontact hypothesis and
rejects it (Feder registered as the standard assessment), and the
corpus's own articles state the category-substitution objection at
full strength before applying the method. The condition-loading
objection — that a reader required to refuse the supernatural and
equipped with astronautics will find astronautics — is carried
against the equipment itself.

**Founder ruling (ADR 0006):** accepted at `contextual`. The corpus
entry's "foundational method" phrasing remains descriptive; elevation
of the relation would be a new founder ruling (the `woh-claim-0023`
precedent), and the record's revision trigger keeps that path named.
