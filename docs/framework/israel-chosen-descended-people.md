# Israel as the creators' chosen and descended people

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, the people of Israel were the humans made by the creator
team whose laboratory lay in the land now called Israel. At a
competition among the creator teams they were judged the most successful
terrestrial type "in intelligence and genius", which the canon gives as
the origin of Israel's chosen-people self-understanding. Through the
unions of the sons of Elohim with the daughters of men, that population
became the Jewish people, whom the canon calls the creators' "direct
descendants on Earth". The canon reconciles its two statements tentatively,
in Raël's commentary voice answering a reader (LWTE-1:17–18). It also narrates Israel's history as a tutelage,
and Israel's keeping of the biblical record as custodianship of the
message. The corpus records all of this as canon-explicit and
mainstream-unendorsed.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label

stage for the `/wiki/hebrews/` entry.

## What the canon says, and where

- **Election.** TBWTT-2:43: « le peuple d'Israël, qui fut lors d'un de ces
  concours… élu par le jury scientifique comme le type humanoïde terrestre
  le plus réussi sur le plan intelligence et génie. Ce qui explique que ce
  peuple s'est toujours considéré comme le peuple élu de Dieu. »
- **Descent.** ETTMTTP-2:104: « les Juifs sont nos descendants directs sur
  la Terre… Ils sont les descendants des fils d'Elohim et des filles des
  hommes dont il est question dans la Genèse. » ETTMTTP-2:123 restates
  this for « le peuple d'Israël ».
- **Harmonization.** LWTE-1:17–18. A reader asks whether the two
  statements contradict each other. The answer: the laboratory-made people
  of Israel was the one elected, and « C'est **peut-être** justement parce
  que ce peuple était le plus réussi que les fils d'Elohim se laissèrent
  tenter par leurs filles… C'est ainsi que la race qui peuplait la terre
  d'Israël devint le peuple juif. »
- **Custody.** TBWTT-4:35 (Israel already knows of the creators);
  ETTMTTP-2:124 (the message kept « jalousement »); TBWTT-5:25 and 5:31
  (the Bible preserved by the Jewish people as testimony).
- **Tutelage.** TBWTT-3:9–10 (liberation); 3:140–142 (dispersion);
  3:225 and 5:24–25 (return as a sign).

## Containment: what this record must never be used for

- **No genetics.** The canon never says "genetic", and no
  population-genetic evidence is asserted. Wiki prose that calls the
  Hebrews "direct genetic descendants" misstates the canon and is outside
  this record.
- **No superiority or individual worth.** "Race" is the canon's 1970s
  wording, not a corpus category. The election clause is recorded as the
  canon's account of a self-understanding, not as a ranking the corpus
  endorses. The ethical objection (alt-0076-ethical) stands at full
  strength.
- **No settled genealogy.** The canon's own « peut-être » is carried.
  Prose must present the election and descent clauses as two canonical
  statements with a tentative harmonization, never as one fixed line.
- **No identity beyond the canon.** The ʿIvrim ↔ "people of Israel"
  relation is the public entry's scope convention: the ancient ʿIvrim are
  incidental, cited history (first occurrence Genesis 14:13; the Eber,
  ʿeber, and habiru/ʿapiru etymologies; outsider usage in Genesis and
  Exodus), and the canon speaks only of « le peuple d'Israël », « les
  Juifs », and « le peuple élu ». The record asserts no identity between
  the canon's people and the ʿIvrim, the habiru, or any archaeological
  population.
- **No State-of-Israel predictions.** The embassy demand and the
  conditional chastisement and destruction statements (ETTMTTP-2:104,
  2:122–127) belong to woh-claim-0015 and woh-claim-0016.
- **Jewish election theology in its own voice.** Deuteronomy 7:6–8 is
  preserved, not superseded, and is carried as the strongest textual
  counter.

## Alternatives and challenges

- Covenant election: Deuteronomy 7:7–8 grounds election in love and
  oath, not merit.
- Ethnogenesis: Israel emerges from an Iron Age I highland population;
  no lineage reaches back to a primordial creation.
- Internal tension: the canon's readers saw a contradiction, and the
  harmonization is tentative.
- Sociological: the emphasis tracks the embassy strategy and the
  founder's paternity account.
- Ethical: ranking peoples by "genius" has a harmful reception history.
