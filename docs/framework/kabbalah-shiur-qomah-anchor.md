# The Kabbalah ranking and its Shi'ur Qomah anchor

## Exact proposition

The canon names the Kabbalah "the book closest to the truth" and cites
two stature figures — 236,000 parasangs, and heels of 30 million — and
both figures are genuinely attested in the Shi'ur Qomah tradition
transmitted within the Hekhalot literature. The corpus reads the
correspondence as the canon engaging a real, identifiable textual
tradition; the light-second gloss is the article's interpretive
development, carried at its own strength.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `comparative` and public
label `inferred`, matching the published Explainer.

## What makes this record unusual

Its middle layer is *checkable and checked at the textual level*: the
figures either occur in the Shi'ur Qomah stream or they do not, and the
Explainer's finding is that they do. That verification — the strongest
kind available to a comparative claim in this catalog — supports the
engagement reading and nothing more: the commonplace-figures alternative
(popularized esoterica rather than tradition-engagement) survives it, and
the tradition's own understanding of the measurements
(liturgical-mystical, with the Maimonidean rejection in its reception)
is preserved against any data-reading, per the record's scope.

The project holds the Shi'ur Qomah in digitization, so deepening the
verification is cheap; the wantlist need not carry this one.

## Alternatives and challenges

Commonplace figures; anthropomorphic-theology function; gloss overreach
(units retrofit and selection effect on the arithmetic).
