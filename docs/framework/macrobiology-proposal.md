# Macrobiology: humanity as organism one scale up

## Exact proposition

Taking literally the canon's axioms — everything in the universe is
living, the Earth like all planets; each human is like a cell of the
great being in gestation that is Humanity — the corpus proposes
**macrobiology**: the study of living systems composed of biological
individuals, one scale above the organism, as microbiology studies the
scale below the cell.

A `model` with framework relation `derived` (from the fractal-scale
frame, `woh-claim-0023`) and public label `speculative`, matching the
public wiki entry's own label.

## Provenance

Distilled at founder direction from `wiki/macrobiology.md` and the Shape
of Humanity Explainer. Deferred from the first distillation batch until
its placement resolved; the resolution adopted is dependency on the
fractal frame — the discipline proposal *derives* from the canon's scale
picture, which is where the foundational weight actually sits.

## The engagement discipline

The adjacent literatures — superorganism biology, the major-transitions
programme, Gaia, Vernadsky's biosphere, the noosphere writers, the
scaling-laws work — are conversation partners, each preserved in its own
terms, and none of them reaches the canon's axiom. The record says so
plainly, and carries the individuality-criteria objection (that the
major-transitions literature the corpus cites actually cuts against
humanity-as-organism) at full strength. The moment the proposal produces
a testable claim, that claim leaves this record and gets its own, at its
own grade.

## Alternatives and challenges

Metaphor-not-ontology; existing-fields-suffice; individuality criteria.
