# The antediluvian civilization

## Exact proposition

The span between the creation and the Flood — Age of Leo through Age of
Cancer, c. 11,375–6,690 BCE on the corpus's compressed chronology — held a
developing human civilization in contact with the exiled creator faction;
its development and hybrid lineages are what the Flood decision was taken
against, and the biblical genealogies with their parallel traditions
preserve the span as the antediluvian age.
