# The inhabited-universe situation

## Exact proposition

The corpus situates the Elohim-civilization hypothesis within
science's own possibility-space: a concrete instance of Crick–Orgel
directed panspermia, against the plurality-of-worlds background from
Greek atomism through the Sagan–Shklovskii survey to the exoplanet
era. The situation is taxonomic, not evidential: the sciences supply
a slot, not a verdict.

An `interpretation`, `contextual`, `inferred`.

## The division of labor and the honesty clause

This record is `woh-claim-0065`'s agent-side twin: that record
establishes mechanism-availability (life can be engineered), this one
agent-category availability (seeding civilizations are a scientific
category, not a contradiction) — both yield coherence for
`woh-claim-0001`, neither yields confirmation, and no probability
argument is run. The honesty clause is registered at full strength:
Crick and Orgel proposed the category tentatively and without the
framework's specifics, and Crick's own later assessment of its
speculative standing is the record's standing control. The
category-comfort objection (a marginal slot lends no respectability)
and the plurality-cuts-both-ways objection (a background that permits
everything selects nothing) are carried in full. The registered
scientific lineage runs Arrhenius (1908 panspermia) → Crick–Orgel
(the directed variant); the mid-century survey literature that fed
Sendy's program sits in the same background — context, not
authority.
