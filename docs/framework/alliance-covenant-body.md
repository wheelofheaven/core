# The Alliance as covenant-born body

## Exact proposition

The Alliance is the corpus's name for the formal relationship between
the Elohim and the Eden-lineage humans: constituted at the post-Flood
Noahic covenant (the biblical covenant read as treaty memory),
persisting through the conflict's settlement — which pardoned the exiles
without unmaking the human covenant — and standing behind the later
engagement institutions.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `speculative` — matching the entry's own
label, because the single named body with continuous existence is corpus
systematization over scattered canonical covenant material, and the
record says so.

## Discipline

No invented institutional detail (charters, offices, rosters); the
Council of the Eternals is a different body (the Elohim's own); covenant
theology's readings stay in their own voice; the return-era institutions
belong to 0015/0016. The over-systematization objection is carried live.
