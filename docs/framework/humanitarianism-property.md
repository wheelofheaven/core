# Humanitarianism as the canon's property doctrine

## Exact proposition

The canon proposes humanitarianism — outright ownership abolished in
favour of forty-nine-year leases, inheritance ended — as the second New
Commandment. The fourth normative record, completing the New
Commandments' institutional core (government 0032, economy 0033, ethics
0041), under the same is/ought policing: reportable, never endorsed or
operationalized.

A `normative_recommendation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The jubilee register

The forty-nine-year term echoes the Levitical jubilee, and the record
registers the echo as the canon's own frame — while excluding arguing
the economics from the scripture, and carrying the jubilee-romanticism
objection (the institution's own historicity and scope are debated; the
echo lends antiquity, not feasibility). The incentive and
capital-formation objections stand at full strength.
