# The compressed chronology of terrestrial life

Drafted at founder direction on 2026-11-22. **Draft, awaiting founder
review.** No public page may be written against this record until it is
accepted.

The founder's position, in his words: "the old age of the earth is legit,
but not that of its life on it"; "all life in the last 25,000 years". He
opposes geological gradualism and holds that the geologic time scale, on
which the standard account of large-scale evolution depends, misdates the
history of life.

## Exact proposition

The dictated Messages report that the Elohim found the Earth already
existing and wholly covered by water, landed about twenty-five thousand
years ago, raised a single continent, and created every form of life on the
planet in succession, from the first single cells to humans.

Wheel of Heaven takes that report as describing what happened. The planet
is old. All terrestrial life, including the forms known only from the
fossil record, falls within roughly the last twenty-five thousand years.

The claim therefore:

- rejects the geologic time scale as a chronology of life;
- reads the fossil sequence as the order of creation and of burial;
- entails that the methods assigning millions of years to fossils are
  mistaken.

Mainstream geology, palaeontology and geochronology reject all three.

It is recorded as a `hypothesis`, `derived` from
[`woh-claim-0001`](elohim-civilization-hypothesis.md) and
[`woh-claim-0070`](canonical-contact-report.md), with the reader-facing
label `framework`.

Two things the dictated text does not say. It gives no figure for how long
the creation took in years, beyond the first book's creation "days" of
about two thousand years each (`TBWTT-2:10`, read with
[`woh-claim-0003`](precessional-world-age-chronology.md)). And it says
nothing about dating methods, fossils as such, or the origin of oil and
coal.

## What the corpus adds

The step from "the Messages report this" to "this is the chronology of
life" is the hypothesis, and it is Wheel's. So are two consequences the
canon does not spell out:

1. **The dating must be wrong.** If trilobites, dinosaurs and mammoths all
   lived within twenty-five thousand years, every method that places them
   millions of years apart misdates them. The claim owes an account of how.
2. **The fossil order is an order of making and of burial.** The sequence
   from simple to complex in the rocks matches the order the Messages give
   for creation; [`woh-claim-0071`](continental-breakup-at-the-flood.md)
   records the reading of the strata as what was buried in the cataclysm.

## Readings from the tradition that are not canon

- **Raël, `LWTE-1:13–15`.** Asked how bones hundreds of thousands of years
  old can exist, Raël answers that an earlier creation, destroyed before
  the Elohim came, may have left them, and that there has been "une
  infinité de créations sur notre planète". This reconciles the Messages
  with old fossils by a different route from this claim, and is listed as
  an alternative.
- **Raël, `LWTE-1:28–31`.** Dating methods wrongly assume that atomic
  behaviour was always as it is now; the continents first moved hundreds
  of kilometres a year. This is the argument against uniform rates in
  Raël's own voice, and the nearest thing in the tradition to the claim's
  first consequence.
- **Terrusse, `LWTE-4:26–54`.** Oil and coal as organic matter buried at
  once; frozen mammoths; dinosaurs not recreated after the Flood.

## Questions to be investigated

None of these is asserted. They are where the claim meets the physical
record, and where it can be tested under the
[research programme](../methodology/research-programme.md).

- **Soft tissue.** Flexible vessels, cells and protein fragments have been
  reported from dinosaur bone. What survives, how often, and what does each
  account predict about it?
- **Radiocarbon.** A fossil millions of years old should hold no measurable
  radiocarbon; one younger than about fifty thousand years should. What do
  measurements on fossil bone and coal show, and how is contamination
  excluded?
- **Rapid burial.** Assemblages buried alive, such as the Mongolian
  "Fighting Dinosaurs". Both accounts expect sudden burial there; what
  would tell them apart?
- **Annual layers.** What are the layers in polar ice and in lake
  sediments counted past twenty-five thousand years, and could a
  cataclysm lay down many in a year?
- **The Flood in phases.** The canon's Flood is a strike that destroys all
  life (`TBWTT-2:58`). A cataclysm of that kind would have phases and local
  events, some without water. What sequence of deposits would it leave?
- **Oil and coal.** The founder's question: whether fossil fuels are
  largely of non-biological origin and only in small part fossil life. The
  dictated Messages are silent. The standard view is that petroleum
  carries molecular remains of organisms.
- **Catastrophist authors.** Hans-Joachim Zillmer proposes that the fossil
  record was sorted by turbulent water in stages of a flood. His books have
  not been inspected by the project; he is to be read as a comparative
  voice, with his evidence checked item by item.

## The boundary with young-earth creationism

The claim shares one thing with young-earth creationism: a history of life
measured in thousands of years. It differs on the rest. The planet is old;
the makers are a civilization, not a deity; the event that buried the
record is a deliberate strike, not a year of rain. Prose should state the
shared point and the differences together.

## Alternatives and challenges

Recorded at their strength in the claim record and the
[evidence map](../evidence/woh-claim-0080-evidence.md):

- concordant radiometric ages and the constancy of decay rates;
- annual-layer records that pass twenty-five thousand years without a
  break and carry signs of life throughout;
- features of the rock record that need long time (reefs, fossil soils,
  successive trackways, evaporites) and a fossil order explained by
  descent;
- Raël's earlier-creations reply;
- the source-critical alternative to the contact report itself.

The second of these is the hardest for the claim, because it does not
depend on any decay rate.
