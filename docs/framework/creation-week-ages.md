# The creation week as precessional sequence

## Exact proposition

Each day of the Genesis 1 creation week is one precessional age of
roughly 2,160 years, in the assignment the timeline chapters carry
under the Model-C lattice — the arrival and orbital survey opening in
the Age of Capricorn, the works proceeding age by age. The
identification supplies exactly what `woh-claim-0039` deliberately
withheld: the day-length arithmetic, at the lattice's precision and
risk.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The join and its risks

This record is the join of two parents and inherits both risk
profiles explicitly: `woh-claim-0039` supplies the staged reading
(days as working periods of a project), `woh-claim-0003` supplies the
lattice (the ages, their boundaries, the anchor). The per-day
assignments live in the timeline chapters, not here — the record
moves with them if the lattice's assignments are revised. Two
exclusions do the disciplinary work: no concordist mapping onto
cosmological or geological deep time (the ages are the corpus's
frame, not an earth-science claim), and no precision beyond the
lattice's own model figure. The double-jeopardy objection — that
day-equals-age is the corpus's neatest and therefore most suspect
equation, inheriting every objection to both parents — is carried in
full, alongside the schema-convenience objection that seven days onto
twelve ages requires editorial choices the text cannot arbitrate.
