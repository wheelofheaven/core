# The threshold choice

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, humanity crossed at the 1945 atomic threshold into the
decisive period of its history: scientifically capable of
self-destruction and, by the same capability, capable of understanding
its origins and reaching its creators' level. The choice between the two
outcomes is the canon's framing of the present era and its stated reason
for initiating contact when it did.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## The coupling is the claim

The distinctive structure is not the danger (mid-century existential-risk
discourse is acknowledged context, and the Cold-War-absorption
alternative is carried) but the **coupling**: one capability underlying
both the threat and the possibility, so that neither can be renounced
without the other. That is why the canon's test is maturity rather than
restraint — and why the record's principal drift line is the Dispatch
genre's standing temptation to read each week's news as movement toward
either pole. The scope excludes that reading in both directions, along
with outcome predictions and policy content (the canon's own proposals
live in the normative records, 0032 and 0033).
