# Fractal cosmology: the infinite in both directions

## Exact proposition

The canon presents a spatially self-similar cosmos: stars and planets as
the atoms of a larger living being, our atoms as inhabited worlds, and in
both directions infinity — with the compressed corollary that time runs
inversely to the mass-scale of the form of life. The corpus adopts this
picture as the canon's cosmological frame. The published Explainer's
dialogue with modern physics is a separate, speculative layer outside
this record.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `foundational` and public
label `framework`.

## Foundational placement — founder-confirmed

This is the first distilled record assigned `foundational`: the scale
picture does not derive from the Elohim-civilization hypothesis or any
other recorded claim — it is its own canonical premise, the frame that
`woh-claim-0025` (macrobiology) derives from. The assignment was flagged
at drafting and **confirmed by the founder in the 2026-08-16 acceptance
ruling (ADR 0005)**. The machine record's dependency on `woh-claim-0020`
records provenance (the premise is canon-delivered), not derivation.

## The boundary against physics

The record's scope excludes any claim of mainstream support, and records
the observational situation plainly: statistical homogeneity at the
largest surveyed scales stands against unbounded upward self-similarity,
and quantum structure stands against the downward direction read
literally. The Explainer's engagement with such material is its own
`speculative` development; nothing in this record licenses citing physics
for the frame.

## Alternatives and challenges

Observational homogeneity; the atomism-poetry genre reading; the
quantum-floor objection.
