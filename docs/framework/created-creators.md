# The created creators

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, the Elohim were themselves created — their civilization
discovered its own designed origin, as its creators had discovered
theirs — an unbounded chain of creation with no first term, coherent
with the infinite cosmos of `woh-claim-0023`. The corpus deploys the
chain as the framework's answer to the who-designed-the-designer
regress: the objection is accepted and absorbed rather than rebutted.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## Provenance

Distilled at founder direction. The Signature draft's anchor scene —
Dawkins conceding the engineered-life hypothetical and immediately
retracting it because "the designer would have had to evolve" — is
precisely where this record does its work: the canon's designers did
evolve, and were also created, and so on without bottom. The chain
converts the regress from refutation into structure.

## The two-premise lock

An unbounded chain requires an unbounded cosmos: this record and
`woh-claim-0023` stand or fall together, and the machine record declares
the dependency. The temporal objection (standard cosmology's finite
past) therefore lands here with full force on top of 0023's
observational ones — recorded, not argued around. The canon's atheism is
constitutive: prose that lets a terminal designer in through the back
door has left the record.

Formulability context: Crick–Orgel shows a created-life hypothesis is
scientifically stateable — the epistemic principles' own example of
formulability-without-evidence, and it is cited as exactly that.

## Alternatives and challenges

Regress-unanswered (postponement forever versus a terminating
abiogenesis somewhere); the cosmological conflict; the theogonic-pattern
reading (creators-with-creators as modernized divine genealogy).
