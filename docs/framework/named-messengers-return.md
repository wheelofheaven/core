# The canon's named messengers and their expected return

## Exact proposition

The canon names specific figures as messengers of the Elohim, at two
voice layers.

- **Dictated message.** At a meal of "the forty or so" on the planet of
  the Eternals, Yahweh presents:
  - Jesus, recreated from a preserved cell;
  - Moses at Jesus's right and Elijah at his left;
  - "the one whom, on Earth, one remembers under the name of Buddha", at
    Jesus's left;
  - Mohammed, "a little further" (`ETTMTTP-2:62–64`).

  He also names Moses, Ezekiel, Elijah, Jesus, Mohammed and Buddha as
  predecessors of "the last of the line of the prophets"
  (`ETTMTTP-3:257`).
- **Raël's commentary voice** (the third book). Raël names Jesus, Moses,
  Buddha, Mohammed and Joseph Smith among the envoys of the creators
  whose return with the Elohim Raëlians await (`LWTE-3:27`). He names
  Moses, Jesus, Buddha and Mohammed as the ancient messengers to be
  welcomed at the embassy (`LWTE-1:87`, `LWTE-3:82`). The same names are
  presupposed by a reader's question at `LWTE-1:75`.

A `source_report`, `contextual`, `direct`.

## The voice rule

Founder direction (2026-11-08): Raël's own commentary voice in the third
book is canon, but every use is marked as commentary, distinct from the
dictated messages. Joseph Smith is therefore "named by Raël among the
envoys". He is never seated at the table, and no figure is given an
ordinal. The table lists only those the text places there.

## The founder-directed identification

By founder direction (2026-11-08; ADR to follow), the corpus identifies
the canon's "Buddha" with Siddhārtha Gautama (Śākyamuni). The canon's own
phrase does not fix the referent: "Buddha" is a title, and the canon says
only "the one whom, on Earth, one remembers under the name of Buddha".
The identification is recorded here as the corpus's, and prose must
present it that way (`alt-0077-buddha-referent`). No other identification
is made. The name Maitreya is excluded, as are Moroni and Kolob.

## What the record does not do

It publishes no roster and assigns no census tier. The roughly-forty
bound and the census discipline belong to `woh-claim-0054`. The record
does not describe per-figure contact or mission (`woh-claim-0038`'s
instance policy), and it does not assert that the scene or the
recreations happened. The return clause only points to
`woh-claim-0015` and `woh-claim-0016`.
