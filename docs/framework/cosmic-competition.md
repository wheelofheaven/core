# Cosmic competition among created humanities

## Exact proposition

Earth's humanity is one of multiple humanities the Elohim created on
multiple worlds, each facing the same maturity evaluation — the
threshold coupling of `woh-claim-0031` — with success inheriting the
creators' level (the forward direction of `woh-claim-0030`'s chain).

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The articulation disclosure

The plurality and the evaluation are canon-stated; "competition" is the
corpus's frame, and the record separates the two — no contest between
humanities is narrated anywhere in the canon, and the frame-overreach
objection (importing game-dynamics into an evaluation) is carried. No
populations, locations, rankings, or Fermi apologetics.
