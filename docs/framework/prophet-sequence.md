# The prophet sequence

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, the prophets were operational interlocutors between the
Elohim and specific peoples and eras — contacted, briefed, tasked, each
delivering the message in the register his era could carry. The sequence
is the mechanism behind the canon's claim that the religions testify to
the same creators (`woh-claim-0027`).

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## Provenance and structure

Distilled at founder direction from `wiki/prophet.md` and the figure
entries (Moses, Elijah, Muhammad). The record operates at collective
level with an instance policy: per-figure records are minted only where
the corpus develops them (`woh-claim-0037` for Jesus is the first), so
figure entries can bind the sequence now and gain instance records
later. The era-adaptation clause carries the traditions' differences as
design rather than error — which is also the clause under most pressure
from the comparative-theology alternative (Islamic prophetology built a
sequence for its own reasons, and the record engages it without
conflation). The Daniel-era emissaries (`woh-claim-0006`) are a
different operational category and are excluded by name.

wherever they are used. The statement, scope, label and alternatives are
unchanged. Per-figure naming is carried by `woh-claim-0077`.
