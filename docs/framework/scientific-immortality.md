# Scientific immortality by cloning and memory transfer

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, the Elohim practice scientific immortality — regrowth
from preserved cells with transfer of memory and personality. It is the
mechanism of the Eternals and the Council, and the mechanism behind the
canon's re-description of resurrection and judgment: the dead recreated
from preserved records, with roughly 8,400 recreated humans on the
Planet of the Eternals. Religious resurrection language is read as
memory of this technology.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## The registered-not-resolved question

A recreation with transferred memories: the same person, or a copy? The
continuity problem is philosophy's hardest standing objection to every
uploading-and-recreation doctrine, and the record registers it without
resolving it — the canon asserts the practice; the metaphysics of
personal identity is not the corpus's to settle, and prose that settles
it casually trips a trigger. Equally excluded: any claim of terrestrial
capability, and the movement's cell-sampling rites (organizational
history, not the claim).

## Alternatives and challenges

The continuity problem; resurrection-as-theology (category substitution
applied here); the doctrine's sociological function.
