# The Ark as communication device

## Exact proposition

The Ark of the Covenant — built at Sinai to exact delivered
specifications, the voice of Yahweh issuing from the kapporet between
the cherubim, lethal to mishandlers — is read, per the canon, as a
communication device linking the Israelite leadership to the alliance:
the specifications, the handling discipline, and the Uzzah casualty
read as preserved operational memory.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## The reconstruction boundary

The record asserts the canon's device reading and refuses the schematic:
the electrical-speculation lineage (the capacitor tradition) is named as
an alternative's home, not adopted, and prose that publishes
reconstructions has left the record. Equally out: the disappearance
question, treasure narratives, and extension to other temple furniture
without records — the artifact class stays one artifact wide until the
corpus develops more. The mainstream cultic account (the empty throne of
the invisible enthroned deity; lethality as holiness theology) is
preserved in its own voice.
