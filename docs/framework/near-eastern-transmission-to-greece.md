# Near Eastern literary transmission to archaic Greece

## Exact proposition

Archaic Greek poetry and myth absorbed substantial Near Eastern —
Mesopotamian, Anatolian, and Levantine — literary material through documented
contact routes, and the wanderings strand of the *Odyssey* stands in an
especially strong and clear literary relationship with the Gilgamesh epic,
without identity of the two heroes.

This is an `empirical_observation` with framework relation `contextual` and
public label `direct`. It records mainstream scholarship's position, at the
strength mainstream scholarship states it, and nothing more.

## Why this record exists

The framework's Greek claims (`woh-claim-0004`, `woh-claim-0007`) deliberately
exclude transmission routes from their scope. This record is where the routes
live: the documented, human, east-to-west movement of stories that any Wheel
reading of Greek material sits on top of and must never contradict. Keeping
the substrate in its own record keeps the framework layer honest — a Wheel
artifact citing this record is citing *scholarship*, and gains no framework
conclusion from it.

## What the observation actually shows

Contact and prestige asymmetry: archaic Greeks absorbed material from cultures
they regarded as older and richer, along routes archaeology documents (the
Phoenician-derived alphabet; Euboean trade presence at Al Mina; Anatolian
festival-epic channels). The Odyssey–Gilgamesh case, verified against West
pp. 402–403 from the page image, is stated by West as an "especially strong
and clear relationship" of the wanderings strand — with character identity
explicitly denied: "He is, then, no Gilgamesh."

**What it does not show.** Literary borrowing does not establish
civilizational origination. Mesopotamian priority in writing and urbanism is
independently true and independently evidenced (archaeology, not philology);
sliding from "the Greeks borrowed stories" to "therefore the Near East
originated civilization" is a resemblance-to-priority transition the claim
model forbids. Any artifact wanting the priority thesis must bind
`woh-claim-0007`, which carries it at framework strength — not this record.
