# The Mass Effect as time-scale law

## Exact proposition

The Mass Effect (Law of Masstime) is the corpus's named articulation of
the canon's compressed sentence: subjective time scales with the mass
and complexity of the living form across the scale hierarchy — larger
beings live proportionally slower.

An `interpretation`, `derived` (from the fractal frame, 0023),
`speculative`.

## The seed-to-construct ratio, disclosed

One canonical sentence; a named "law" built on it. The record states
that ratio plainly and builds its walls accordingly: no relativistic or
metabolic-scaling support may be claimed (those literatures measure
different things, and adjacency is not articulation — the record's own
sharpest objection); no exponent or constant is asserted; the frame
itself stays 0023's. What the construct earns its keep doing is making
the frame's living-scales picture coherent — a galaxy-scale being's
moment spanning our aeons — and nothing more.
