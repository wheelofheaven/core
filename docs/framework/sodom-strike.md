# The Sodom strike

## Exact proposition

Genesis 18-19 read as a late-Taurus targeted strike by the home-world
Council against an organized human vengeance movement — the post-Babel
dispersed elite's descendants, regathered at the Cities of the Plain
and preparing an expedition against the creators. Two scouts verify
the threat, Abraham negotiates the threshold, Lot's family is
extracted, and the strike falls: a rebellion suppressed, against
Babel's peace overture dispersed and the Flood's planetary reset —
three instruments of one containment policy at three scales.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The dependencies and the discipline

The record's two dependencies are structural: `woh-claim-0017`
supplies the rebels (the episode makes no sense without the post-Babel
dispersal it reverses), and `woh-claim-0022` receives the episode's
named consequence — the reversal of the Serpent symbolism from
positive emblem to negative, which the corpus reads as feeding the
later Devil conflation. The disciplinary exclusion is geological: the
Dead Sea basin is carried as the reading's claimed trace at
*consonance* grade, with the mainstream account (tectonic origin on
the Dead Sea Transform, evaporative salinity) registered against it
in full — the basin is never evidence for the strike, and no site
archaeology or airburst thesis is enlisted. The reception history's
sexual-ethics reading is left to the tradition; the corpus reads
political-military rebellion.
