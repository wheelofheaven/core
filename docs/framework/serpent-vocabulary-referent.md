# The serpent vocabulary as faction memory

## Exact proposition

The Hebrew Bible's serpentine vocabulary — *nachash*, *tannin*,
*liwyatan* — is read as the memory vocabulary through which the tradition
preserves the exiled creator faction: the disclosing figure of Eden and
the pursued exiles of the conflict. The chaos-serpent and wisdom-serpent
strands are kept distinct, and the case rests on no etymology.

## The strand discipline

The chaos-dragon and wisdom-serpent clusters are different iconographic
families — the distinction the corpus verified against Black & Green's
entries (the catalog's one `excerpt`-grade scholarly verification, logged
in the holdings registry). The referent reading must respect that
distinction per passage; a record-level flattening of the two strands is
a revision trigger.
