# Samsara re-read through recreation

## Exact proposition

The canon re-reads the Dharmic rebirth cycle through its recreation
mechanism: in the second message the cycle is rendered scientifically —
recreation from records rather than metaphysical transmigration —
joining resurrection (`woh-claim-0029`) among the structures the canon
demystifies into one technology.

An `interpretation`, `comparative`, `framework`.

## The frame-mismatch honesty

The record carries its sharpest objection by name: *anatta* makes "the
same being recreated" precisely what Buddhist philosophy denies, so the
mapping fits the frame it borrows worse than any other — recorded, not
smoothed. The traditions' irreducible articulations (karma theory,
liberation goals) are preserved; karma's moral accounting and liberation
itself are outside scope, because the canon does not re-describe them
and the corpus will not invent it.
