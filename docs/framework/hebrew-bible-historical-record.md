# The Hebrew Bible as compressed historical record

## Exact proposition

The framework's global reading posture: the Tanakh read not as religious
myth but as a compressed, transmitted, theologically reworked historical
record of the creators' engagement — with the transmission disciplines
(composition history, redaction, Masoretic transmission, translation
loss, the memory sciences' loss characteristics) conceded at full
strength and binding on every reading made under the posture.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The distribution rule — this record's whole point

The posture licenses **no specific reading**. Every passage-level claim
has or needs its own record, and this record may never be cited as
evidence for any of them. The record names its own failure mode —
posture-laundering, where ambient framing lends support the record
formally withholds — as a revision trigger: an artifact citing 0053 for
a specific reading is the violation this record exists to make visible.
Inerrancy is excluded in both directions; genre differences in
memory-carrying are per-genre editorial work; other corpora need their
own posture records.
