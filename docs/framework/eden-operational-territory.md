# Eden as the Israel team's operational territory

## Exact proposition

Eden is read, per the canon, as the operational territory of the Israel
team of Elohim creators — the site of the biological-synthesis work, of
the disclosure episode, and of the expulsion. The corpus's placement of
that territory on the antediluvian supercontinent is a geographical
inference riding on the canonical reading, and is labelled as such inside
the record.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`. The public wiki page carries `inferred` — a defensible page
call reflecting the geographical layer; the record's label follows the
main (canonical) claim, and the two grades are kept visible.

## Provenance

Distilled at founder direction from `wiki/eden.md` (21 in-corpus links).
The opening episode of the conflict carried by `woh-claim-0008`.

## Two layers

1. **Canonical (framework):** the team's territory, the garden, the
   scientific books, the expulsion — TBWTT chapter 2's narrated sequence.
2. **Geographical (inferred, labelled):** the supercontinent placement,
   from the corpus's reconstruction, going beyond the canon's statements
   and carrying its own alternative (supercontinent-overreach) at full
   strength.

Excluded outright: modern-location identifications and the rivers of
Genesis 2 as a solvable map — the two perennial temptations of Eden
literature.

## Alternatives and challenges

The wisdom-aetiology reading; the literary Mesopotamian setting; and the
internal overreach objection to the geographical layer.
