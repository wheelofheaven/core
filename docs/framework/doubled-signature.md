# The Doubled Signature

## Exact proposition

Each precessional age encodes its astronomical signature doubly — in the
age's own zodiacal emblem and a paired counter-form — so iconographic
age-attribution can be cross-checked rather than free-associated. An
extension of Hamlet's Mill, long reserved by `woh-claim-0014` and minted
here from its own entry.

An `interpretation`, `comparative`, `speculative`.

## The instrument reading

The principle's value is stated as *constraint*: an attribution that
cannot show both signatures is weakened by the corpus's own rule, and
free-association rescue (loosening the pairing to save a reading) is
excluded. The flexibility objection — that two signatures give more
matching surface unless pairings are fixed in advance — is the record's
live internal threat, and per-age pairing fixation in corpus practice is
its first trigger. The Mithraic tauroctony is the exhibit case. Ancient
iconographers are not claimed to have held the principle.
