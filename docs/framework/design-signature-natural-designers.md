# The design signature and the natural designers

## Exact proposition

The corpus engages the design-inference debate on a third footing: it
takes the signature intuition seriously (Meyer's programme; Dawkins's
on-camera hypothetical) while rejecting the ID movement's supernatural
designer — the canon's designers are natural, evolved elsewhere,
themselves created (`woh-claim-0030`), and the hypothesis is
scientifically formulable in the way Crick–Orgel demonstrated. The
corpus's claim is coherence and stateability; its honesty clause is
that **no biological evidence currently discriminates designed from
evolved terrestrial life**.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `comparative` and public
label `speculative`.

## The two walls

The record is built between two walls it may not lean past. Against the
ID movement: no endorsement of its arguments, institutions, or designer
— the corpus's position is a departure from Meyer at the hinge, not an
alliance. Against overclaim: no detection assertion, ever — the
synthetic-genome work shows design of life is an existing engineering
practice (possibility), and possibility is where the record stops. The
discriminating-test trigger cuts both ways: a test that survives review
moves this record whichever way it points.

## Alternatives and challenges

No-signature (the appearance of design is what evolution explains);
unfalsifiable-designers (naturalizing without evidence does not
empiricize); borrowed respectability (the patronizing-parasitism
critique aimed at this exact citation pattern).
