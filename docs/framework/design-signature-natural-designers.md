# The design signature and the natural designers

## Exact proposition

The canon's premise is stated directly: terrestrial life, humanity
included, was designed and engineered by the Elohim (`woh-claim-0001`).
On that footing the corpus engages the design-inference debate: it
reports, in their own voice and with the replies, the scientists who
have argued that life may have been deliberately seeded (Crick and
Orgel's directed panspermia; Crick's *Life Itself*) or that its
unguided origin is mathematically improbable, usually alongside
panspermia (Hoyle and Wickramasinghe); it takes Meyer's signature
programme seriously at the hinge; and it rejects the ID movement's
supernatural designer — the canon's designers are natural, evolved
elsewhere, themselves created (`woh-claim-0030`). Its honesty clause:
**mainstream biology does not accept that design has been detected; a
minority of scientists have argued for deliberate seeding or for the
improbability of an unguided origin, and the corpus reports them as such.**

An `interpretation` with framework relation `comparative` and public
label `speculative`.

## Provenance

Distilled at founder direction from the draft Explainer *The Signature
and the Designers* (the Meyer-rebuttal hinge), pre-grounded ahead of
publication. The regress absorption is `woh-claim-0030`'s work,
declared as a dependency; the formulability citation is the epistemic
principles' own worked example (directed panspermia shows an idea is
formulable without showing the event happened) applied at the exact
place it was written for.

## The two walls

The record is built between two walls it may not lean past. Against the
ID movement: no endorsement of its arguments, institutions, or designer
— the corpus's position is a departure from Meyer at the hinge, not an
alliance. Against overclaim: the minority scientific arguments for
artificiality or improbability are reported as what they are, a
minority position with published replies, never as established science
or consensus; the synthetic-genome work shows design of life is an
existing engineering practice (possibility), not that it happened. The
discriminating-test trigger cuts both ways: a test that survives review
moves this record whichever way it points.

## Alternatives and challenges

No-signature (the appearance of design is what evolution explains);
unfalsifiable-designers (naturalizing without evidence does not
empiricize); borrowed respectability (the patronizing-parasitism
critique aimed at this exact citation pattern).
