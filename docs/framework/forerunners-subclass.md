# Forerunners within the prophet lineage

## Exact proposition

Within the prophet sequence (`woh-claim-0038`), the corpus distinguishes
a preparatory sub-class — forerunners, whose missions prepare the way
for a more central figure, John the Baptist before Jesus as the type
case — inside the roughly forty-prophet lineage the corpus tracks.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `speculative`.

## The riding discipline

Everything here rides on 0038: a forerunner is a sequence member with a
preparatory tasking, nothing more. The forty-count is the corpus's
census of its own tracking, disclosed as such; the sub-class carries no
canonical status; membership beyond actually-classed figures is not
adjudicated. The gospel-typology objection — that the category
generalizes the gospels' own Elijah-typology for the Baptist — is the
record's live critique.
