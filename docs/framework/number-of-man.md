# The Number of Man as generation count

## Exact proposition

Per the third book, 666 (Revelation 13:18) is read as a count, not a
cipher: the number of human generations from the first finalized humans
to the 1945 threshold — tying the Apocalypse's most notorious number to
the era-opening of `woh-claim-0031`.

An `interpretation`, `derived`, `framework`.

## The arithmetic discipline

The count's dependence on the corpus's chronology (0003) is declared;
what the record forbids is *publishing verification arithmetic* —
generation-length assumptions are elastic enough to hit any target, and
the elasticity objection is carried as the reading's standing critique.
The Nero-gematria account (with its 616 manuscript variant) remains the
mainstream reading of the text's composition-era meaning, preserved in
its own voice. No other apocalyptic numbers ride in; no dates come out.
