# The Four Levels as the canon's weighing rule

## Exact proposition

The canon proposes a rule for weighing any situation at four levels —
against infinity, against the Elohim, against human society, against
the individual self — with love as the constant across all four.

A `normative_recommendation` with framework relation `derived` and
public label `framework` — the third normative record, under the same
is/ought policing as the geniocracy and paradism pair.

## Recorded, not deployed

The corpus may report the rule; it may never *use* it as an adjudicator
in its own prose, and it records no casuistry — worked cases would
speak for their authors, not for the canon. The infinity level is what
ties the rule to the framework rather than leaving it a free-standing
maxim: it presupposes the cosmological frame of `woh-claim-0023`, and
the record declares that dependency. The underdetermination objection
(four considerations without a priority rule decide nothing) is carried
as the standing analytical critique.
