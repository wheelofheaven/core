# The post-scarcity lineage convergence

## Exact proposition

A century and a half of terrestrial post-scarcity thought is read as one
intellectual family whose direction converges with the canon's economic
proposals (0033, 0049). The convergence is reception context at
`inferred` grade — availability of the idea in terrestrial thought, in
the exact discipline of `woh-claim-0042` — with no causation either way
and no enrollment of the family's members.

An `interpretation`, `contextual`, `inferred` — pre-grounding the
Jubilee and the Machine Explainer (binds on publication).

## The distinctions that keep it honest

The canon did not source the family; the family does not confirm the
canon; the family's members hold their own positions; the movement's
cyberparadism stream is the family's corpus-adjacent end and stays
distinct from the canon's statements per 0033's vocabulary rule. The
triviality objection — abundance-wishing is universal, so convergence
with it registers nothing — is the standing critique the family-reading
must beat with specificity, not volume.
