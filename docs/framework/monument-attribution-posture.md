# The monument-attribution posture

## Exact proposition

Per-site registration at each thesis's own grade for the
alternative-archaeology complex — the Orion correlation and the Sphinx
redating as the strongest cases, lost-builder generalizations and
sacred-geometry as speculative — with mainstream archaeology and the
skeptical archaeoastronomy controls (Krupp above all) carried in full.
No site, alignment, or redating is evidence for the framework.

An `interpretation`, `contextual`, `inferred`.

## The independence declaration

The posture's point in one sentence: the corpus's chronology stands on
the precessional anchors of `woh-claim-0003` and would survive the
failure of every thesis in this cluster. Where a thesis touches the
age-lattice (a Leo-era monument reading), the touch is registered as
*consonance, never support* — the distinction that keeps the corpus
from mortgaging its chronology to excavation disputes it cannot decide.
No 'ancient mysteries' merging; no construction-impossibility
arguments; the genre-gravity objection (distance-with-coverage is still
coverage) is carried against the posture itself.
