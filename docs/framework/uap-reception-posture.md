# The UAP-era reception posture

## Exact proposition

Registration without enlistment: the modern UAP complex — disclosure
claims, alleged cover-ups, crop formations, and the *documented*
post-1945 secrecy machinery — is none of it evidence for the framework,
whose anticipated contact is specific, official, and conditional
(`woh-claim-0015`) and whose provenance rests on the reports of
`woh-claim-0020`, not on any craft in any sky.

An `interpretation`, `contextual`, `inferred` — pre-grounding the
Reporter at the Razor's Edge Explainer and governing the corpus's UAP
Dispatch coverage (the PURSUE series operates under this record).

## The three grades inside one posture

Documented secrecy (what FOIA journalism has actually established) is
historical fact at documentary strength. Undocumented cover-up and
attribution claims are contested claims at their own grade, skeptical
accounts in full. And the separation cuts both ways: no UAP event
confirms the framework, no debunking refutes it. The
convenient-firewall objection — that this insulates the framework from
the one evidence stream its subject invites — is carried as the
record's sharpest critique, answered only by the one exception the
second trigger names: an official, verified disclosure event is the one
development the separation does not firewall.

The milieu-dependence objection (the movement's UFO-culture history
against the corpus's distance) is preserved; the journalists are not
enlisted.
