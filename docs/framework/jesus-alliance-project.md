# Jesus as alliance project

## Exact proposition

Per the canon, Jesus was a deliberate alliance project: a son of Yahweh
and a human mother, prepared and accompanied for the mission of carrying
the creators' message in the register his era could bear — healings read
as advanced medicine, the resurrection read through the recreation
mechanism (`woh-claim-0029`), and a role announced for the return era.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## Provenance and discipline

Distilled at founder direction from `wiki/jesus.md`. The first
per-figure record of the prophet sequence (`woh-claim-0038`), under its
discipline. Two boundaries do the work: the historical-critical Jesus is
the mainstream account, preserved in its own voice and uncontested on
its own ground; and Christian theology (divinity, atonement, trinity)
is neither target nor content — the framework reading is not a
Christology, and the euhemerized-Christology objection (that the reading
translates the theology it replaces) is carried as an alternative.
Gospel-episode historicity stays per-passage editorial work.
