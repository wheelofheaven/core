# Genesis 1 as compressed creation report

## Exact proposition

The seven-day account of Genesis 1 is read, per the canon's decoded
reading — with Sendy's 1963 literalist statement as modern precedent —
as a compressed report of staged creation work: days as long working
periods, the account's vocabulary as observational, the works as
project stages culminating in the human synthesis.

An `interpretation` with framework relation `derived` and public label
`framework`.

## The anti-concordism clause

The record's sharpest exclusion is aimed at its own genre's failure
mode: staged readings of Genesis 1 historically slide into concordism —
mapping the days onto Big Bang chronology, computing day-lengths,
harmonizing verse by verse with cosmology. The record forbids all of
it: the reading maps the text onto the *project's stages* and nothing
else. The mainstream literary-critical account (P's liturgical
cosmology, the shared ancient Near Eastern vocabulary) is preserved in
its own voice, and the documentary observation is context, not target.

The reading's page in the corpus is the `wiki/the-truth.md` entry (the
canon's decoded-Genesis chapter); the completion joint hands the human
stage to `woh-claim-0018` and the comparative anthropogony to
`woh-claim-0034`.
