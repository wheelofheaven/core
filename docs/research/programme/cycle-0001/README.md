## Notes at registration

- **Families.** `0001` and `0006` apply one test (are the speaker's
  statements about Hebrew words lexically supported?) to two books. `0002`,
  `0003` and `0004` apply one test (is the word a reading rests on in the
  Hebrew, or only in the French rendering?) to three sets of quotations.
  Outcomes within a family are instances of one result.
- **Where the discrimination comes from.** Five of the six `partial`
  ratings rest on the source-critical alternative alone. The theological
  and historical-critical alternatives predict the same result or are
  silent for every biblical-text target, as the claim's evidence map
  already observes.
- **`0005` carries an auxiliary assumption** the claim does not state (that
  the designers left structure in the genetic code beyond what function
  requires). The Deriver's declared prior is that it will not hit.
- **Controls.** `0009` and `0010` are deliberate controls. `0007` and
  `0008` have discrimination `none` on analysis: no alternative commits to
  whether the three books agree with each other.
- **Scope.** Canon expectations test report content, which claim 0001
  excludes in favour of `woh-claim-0070`. They were derived from the
  hypothesis step (the reports describe one real referent). Whether `0007`
  and `0008` belong under 0070 is a question for the ruling.
