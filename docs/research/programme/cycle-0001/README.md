## What the cycle found

These are the things now known that were not in core before. Each is a
report by a Locator or Critic with its passage quoted in the linked file.
Four were re-read against the library by the Recorder (marked ✓). None is
an accepted finding.

1. **The 1979 book limits the creation to life now living.** LWTE-1:15, in
   Raël's voice: « la vie qui se trouve actuellement Terre n'est pas la
   première qui y fut créée… Il y a eu une infinité de créations sur notre
   planète ». Claim 0001 says the Elohim designed "terrestrial life"
   without that limit. ✓ (`0008`)
2. **The canon uses "natural selection" of present humans.** LWTE-1:107:
   « le fruit d'une longue sélection naturelle », of survivors of epidemics
   and wars, with no change of kind. ✓ (`0008`)
3. **One reading reports a verse as saying what it does not say.**
   TBWTT-3:71 gives Joshua 10:13 as saying the war lasted « presque un jour
   entier »; in the verse the phrase is said of the sun. ✓ (`0001`)
4. **The first book never quotes Genesis 2:7.** The claim's evidence map
   has a subclaim D row on `GEN-WOH-2:7` (dust, the potter's verb) with no
   locator for where the canon reads that verse. (`0004`)
5. **One reading rests on a sense the French word has and the Hebrew
   lacks.** TBWTT-2:30 turns « science » in Genesis 2:17 into « livres
   scientifiques »; the Hebrew is *da'at*, knowledge. ✓ A parallel at
   TBWTT-2:42 (Gen 3:22) is unchecked. (`0004`)
6. **A pun that works only in French.** TBWTT-3:192 proposes « sains » for
   « saints » at Ezekiel 42:14. (`0001`)
7. **The commentary is narrative, not lexical.** Across 22 quotations in
   three scopes, 13 have no word taken up at all. The readings rest on the
   step from the verse's image to a technical object, which no lexicon
   test reaches. (`0002`–`0004`)
8. **Outside the *Elohim* gloss, neither of the first two books states
   what a Hebrew word means.** (`0001`, `0006`)
9. **Not checked, flagged for later.** The book's Psalm 19:5 has « sur la
   mer » where the Masoretic text has "in them" (`0003` contest). The
   meaning given for « RAËL » at TBWTT-5:66 was not tested (`0001`).
10. **Library and edition notes.**
    - ETTMTTP-2:71 prints the Ezekiel reference as chapter XXVII; the
      words are in chapter 37. Printing or digitization is not
      established.
    - The LWTE digitization follows an edition of 2000 or later (footnotes
      at LWTE-1:21, 3:26).
    - The `speaker` field is unreliable: every LWTE paragraph is

## Rulings requested

Outcomes available: `hit`, `miss`, `narrowed`, `open`, `void`. Families
are ruled together.

### Family A: does the speaker get the Hebrew right?

#### woh-exp-0001

- **Question.** Which statements does the first book's non-human speaker
  make about the meaning of a Hebrew or biblical word, or about what the
  original text says, and how does each compare with a lexicon and the
  Hebrew?
- **Finding.** Ten statements: 1 supported, 5 unsupported, 4 not
  checkable. [Finding](woh-exp-0001-finding.md).
- **Assessment.** `meets_miss` (at least one unsupported).
- **Critic: sustain, on a narrower basis.** None of the five rows states
  what a Hebrew word means. The miss rests firmly on Joshua 10:13 alone,
  and that is a misreport of the French Bible the book quotes. Two rows
  depend on reading decisions; two were mis-scoped.
  [Contest](woh-exp-0001-contest.md).
- **The ruling turns on** whether "what the original text says" covers any
  report of a verse's wording (then `miss`, narrowly), or only statements
  that invoke the Hebrew or the original (then nothing checkable remains,
  and `narrowed` or `void` fits better). The speaker says the text is
  deformed and that he will explain it (TBWTT-2:2, 2:3, 3:69); whether the
  expectation assumed a kind of philology the book does not claim to do is
  the founder's judgement.
- **Touches.** A `miss` or `narrowed` ruling is material for a review of
  how public pages present the canon's biblical readings. It does not
  touch claim 0001's statement.

#### woh-exp-0006

- **Question.** The same, for the second book.
- **Finding.** No statement about a Hebrew word's meaning in 344
  paragraphs. Four statements of what a biblical text says: 3 supported, 1
  not checkable. [Finding](woh-exp-0006-finding.md).
- **Assessment.** `meets_hit`.
- **Critic: downgrade.** The three items are a copied quotation, a mention
  and an allusion. The honest answer is "no such statement", the
  registered partial case. The hit arose from an inclusion rule the other
  Locator did not use. [Contest](woh-exp-0006-contest.md).
- **The ruling turns on** whether plain quotations count as statements
  about the original text. One rule should govern both books.

### Family B: is the word a reading rests on in the Hebrew?

protocol compared the French translator's word with the Hebrew word, which

### woh-exp-0005

- **Finding.** `unrebutted_unreplicated`: no independent peer-reviewed

  The paper should not be cited as detection.

## What the pilot showed about the method

For the review of RFC 0009.

**Worked.**

- The Critics changed five of ten assessments and narrowed a sixth. The
  mechanical scores alone would have reported five hits.
- Locators reported against the grain without being asked to: the `0001`
  Locator returned five unsupported statements, and all three hinge-word
  Locators independently said the test did not reach what the readings
  rest on.
- The most useful results were by-products, found because whole books
  were read against a neutral question (items 1–6 above).

**Did not work.**

- **Discrimination was overstated at registration.** Five of six
  `partial` ratings did not survive. A rival marked `different` must be
  one that predicts a different result on this target as the protocol
  measures it, and the Deriver's rating needs a check before registration.
- **A design that can only discriminate by failing** (Family B) should be
  caught at registration.
- **Two Locators on one family used different inclusion rules.** A family
  needs one Locator or a protocol tight enough to bind both.
- **Selection rules were underspecified** ("same or following paragraph"
  when the book comments from the paragraph before).
- **A `partial` criterion can hide a miss** (`0005`: the statement's first
  half was false and the label was partial).
- **Scope.** Canon-consistency expectations have no claim to attach to.
  Either `woh-claim-0070` widens, or such expectations are registered as
  source-report work outside a claim's scoreboard.
- **Novelty** was judged against core and missed what the library's own
  edition notes and neighbouring claims already record.
- **Cost.** About 2.1 million agent tokens for ten expectations, most of it
  in reading whole books three times over for one family.

**Proposed amendments, not applied.** A pre-registration check of
discrimination by a second agent; a rule that an expectation must name a
result that would favour the claim over a rival; one Locator per family;
controls kept out of the scoreboard; a `source_report` intake that does
not score against a claim.
