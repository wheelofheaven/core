## The strongest objection

**1. The derivation rested on a hinge the book does not use.**

The derivation says the report "reads the Genesis creation verses as laboratory work", cites the evidence map's hinge at Genesis 2:7 (a fashioning verb with a material), and expects "every hinge of that reading" to be in the Hebrew. The Deriver's declared prior was that the book reads "dust" as chemical material from the soil. The book never quotes Genesis 2:7. On the three creation quotations it does give (plants, water animals and birds, land animals) the commentary takes up no word. It narrates: « Ils ont alors créé ... des cellules végétales à partir de rien d'autre que des produits chimiques ». The laboratory reading of Genesis 1 is laid beside the verses, and it is not drawn out of their wording.

There is more. In both verses the Hebrew makes the land the grammatical subject (תַּדְשֵׁא הָאָרֶץ, תּוֹצֵא הָאָרֶץ), and so does the French (« Que la terre produise », « Que la terre fasse sortir »). The commentary makes the visitors the subject and passes over both verbs. At the point where the wording of the verse and the reading part company, the commentary does not engage the wording at all. The registered test has no class for this. It records "no hinge word" and moves on.

So the count fell short because the word-level exegesis the expectation presupposed is mostly absent from the target, and the Hebrew had no part in it. I do not think this is a careless derivation in the sense of a claim misread. It is a premise about the source, taken from the evidence map and from general impression, that inspection did not bear out. The consequence for the map is noted under "What the ruling turns on".

**2. The two classified words are the control over again, and every alternative predicted them.**

The only hinge words in the core set are « image » (צֶלֶם) and « ressemblance » (דְּמוּת) at Genesis 1:26. `woh-exp-0010` in this cycle is a registered control on the first of them, with `discrimination: none`. The record for `0004` already marks the theological and historical-critical alternatives as predicting `same`. The source-critical alternative, in its proponents' form, predicts `same` as well: an author working from a literal French Genesis will find « image » and « ressemblance » there because the Hebrew has them. No recorded alternative predicts a different result for these two words. The registered `partial` discrimination has nothing to stand on for the result obtained.

Whether the two count as hinge words at all is itself a decision. The commentary (« A notre image! Vous pouvez constater que la ressemblance est frappante. ») explains them as visible bodily likeness, which is not among the protocol's listed kinds (object, material, device, sound, light, movement, procedure). The Locator flags this. If they are excluded the core set has no hinge word and the `partial` is satisfied vacuously.

**3. The borderline set is outside the statement, and counting it changes the class.**

The statement covers quotations "on the making of plants, animals or the human". Genesis 2:16-17 is a command about trees. The Locator was right to set it apart, and the Recorder right to assess on the core set. But the reader should see what the borderline set does under the registered criteria:

| Set counted | Classified | Outcome under the criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core only | 2 `carried` | partial (fewer than four) |
| Core only, « image »/« ressemblance » excluded as not of a listed kind | 0 | partial, vacuously |
| Core and borderline, as the Locator classed them | 4 `carried`, 1 `shifted` | partial (exactly one in five) |
| Core and borderline, « arbre » and « manger » not counted | 2 `carried`, 1 `shifted` | miss (one in three) |

The last row is not a stretch. The Locator's own note says of « arbre » → « livres » and « manger » → « apprendre, lire » that "the substitution itself is not a lexical matter on either side". They are allegory. To enter them as `carried` because the Hebrew for "tree" means tree is to let the class absorb exactly the cases in which the reading has left the word behind. They sit in the tally as two confirmations and dilute the one case that carries information.

**4. The `shifted` case is the family's one discriminating datum, and it points away from the expectation.**

« l'arbre de la science du bien et du mal » is the traditional French phrase, and « science » there has its older sense, knowledge. The Hebrew is הַדַּעַת, which BDB treats in this verse as a verbal noun, "the knowing good and evil". The commentary reads « livres scientifiques », and the paragraph before speaks of « grands secrets scientifiques ». That step is available from the French word in its modern sense. It is not available from דַּעַת. This is what the source-critical alternative said would be found "in tendency": a reading that leans on the French where French and Hebrew part. It is one case. It lies outside the registered scope, and « science » recurs in the book's quotation of Genesis 3:22 (TBWTT-2:42), which nobody has checked. It should not be made to carry more than that, and it should not be lost in a "4 carried, 1 shifted" line.

**5. Novelty and independence.** Registered as `retrodiction`, correctly. The target verses are already cited under this claim and under `woh-claim-0039` and `0073`; the translation's literalness is `woh-claim-0066`. Nothing in the `carried` entries is new to core.

## What the result does and does not show

It shows:

- The book quotes Genesis 1-2 thirteen times, all in TBWTT-2:4 to 2:31; four of the quotations concern the making of plants, animals or the human; Genesis 2:7 is never quoted.
- At Genesis 1:26 the two words the commentary takes up are in the Hebrew, as every reading of the verse expects.
- The creation-of-life commentary is narrative placed beside abridged verses. The explicit word-glosses in this chapter fall on the verses outside the filter: « Elohim », « créé » (« découvert en réalité »), « jour », the upper and lower waters.
- Outside the registered scope, one reading (Genesis 2:17) rests on a sense of the French word that the Hebrew word does not have.

It does not show:

- A "Hebrew footing" for the laboratory reading of the creation verses. The commentary does not tie that reading to any word of them.
- Anything that favours the claim over any recorded alternative.

**The family taken together** (`woh-exp-0002`, `0003`, `0004`). One test, three targets, one result with three instances. In the registered scopes 22 quotations were examined; 13 have no hinge word; 11 hinge words were classified, all `carried` (4, 5, 2). All three Locators independently reported that the commentary seldom takes up a word of the verse, and that where the French word is carried by the Hebrew the technological re-description is a lexicon sense of neither. The registered test compared the translator's word with the Hebrew word, which measures the translation. The family result is that the translation is faithful where the commentary touches it, as `woh-claim-0066` already implied, and that the readings are not lexical. The only case in the family where French and Hebrew part in a way the commentary uses is the « science » case above. Read as one result, the family yields no discriminating hit and one small observation on the source-critical side.

## What the ruling turns on

1. Whether the scope is held to the statement ("on the making of"). If so the instance is a thin null: two words, both already covered by the control `0010`. `partial` as registered or `open` both describe it. If the borderline set is admitted, the founder must also decide whether « arbre » and « manger » are hinge words with lexical content; if they are not, the registered criteria give `miss`.
2. Whether `discrimination: partial` is kept for a result all four alternatives predicted or were silent on.
3. Whether the absence of Genesis 2:7 from the book is taken up outside this ruling. The evidence map's subclaim D row pairs `GEN-WOH-2:7` with the canonical interpretation "directed biological synthesis from raw material". If the first book makes no reading of that verse, the row needs a locator for where the canon does, or a note that it does not. That is a matter for the map's maintainers under review, and a ruling here does not change it.
4. Whether the « science » / דַּעַת observation, with the unchecked parallel at TBWTT-2:42, is registered as its own expectation in a later cycle instead of being scored here, where it is out of scope.
