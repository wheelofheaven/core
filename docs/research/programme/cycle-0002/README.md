# Cycle 0002: French-wording dependence in the first book

Opened 2026-11-22 at the founder's direction, following
[cycle-0001](../cycle-0001/README.md). Claim: `woh-claim-0001` at 0.2.1.

State: ruled 2026-11-22: **`miss`**, falling on the auxiliary premise. The speaker teaches through the reader's French Bible; the French-wording readings (TBWTT-2:30, 2:42, 2:62, 3:192) and Psalm 19:5 belong to the quotation layer, `woh-claim-0066`, not to claim 0001.

- **Finding.** 166 quotations in chapters 2–3 (two known cases excluded): 150 re-describe the thing named, 3 have no explanation, 13 depend on a sense of a French word. Of the 13: 8 `shared`, 2 `french_only` (TBWTT-2:42 « science », Gen 3:22; TBWTT-2:62 « étages », Gen 6:16), 1 disputed (« jour »), 2 the *Elohim* gloss itself. [Finding](woh-exp-0011-finding.md).
- **Assessment.** `meets_miss`.
- **Critic: downgrade.** TBWTT-2:42 is the known 2:30 reading recurring; the new case, « étages », is borderline between a French polysemy and the ark-as-rocket image. Miss and formal hit are two judgement calls apart, and the framework reading also expects this result, so the miss falls first on the auxiliary assumption. [Contest](woh-exp-0011-contest.md).
- **The ruling turns on** whether the speaker's explanations are meant to rest on the original text (then the miss bears on the claim's reading of the speaker) or teach through the listener's French Bible (then it bears on the auxiliary premise and on claim 0066); whether 2:42 counts as a further instance; and whether « étages » is polysemy or image.
- **Adjacent.** Psalm 19:5 is quoted with « sur la mer », absent from the Masoretic text (בָּהֶם); it follows a conjectural emendation and belongs to the quotation layer (claim 0066).
