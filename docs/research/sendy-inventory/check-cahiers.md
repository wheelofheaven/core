An earlier project note said the EPUB with this name held *Nous autres, gens du Moyen Âge*. That warning does not apply to this file.

Almost all the overlap is in **Genesis 1–11**: Cahier 2, ch. III–VII, where Sendy runs a "Théosite" colonisation reading. Exodus to Kings is barely touched. **Ezekiel, Daniel, Jonah, Zechariah, Job and Psalms 8/19 do not appear at all**, so I100–I135 are all absent. The Gospels appear only as precession symbolism or as physics (thermodynamic) allegory.

## Strongest `same` items
- **I008**: each creation "day" is one zodiacal era of about 2,160 years ("C'est le 6e « jour », Ere située entre — 11 010 et — 8850").
- **I006**: the spirit over the waters is astronauts circling a fog-bound Earth.
- **I012**: the luminaries verse is the colonists setting up a terrestrial astronomy.
- **I015**: herbivores, then reptiles, then carnivores, for ecological balance.
- **I019 / I021**: the tree of knowledge is the colonists' science, forbidden to the natives. The serpent is a dissident colonist ("M. Satan") who held that humans had a right to the Initiation.
- **I029**: the sons of the Elohim interbreed with human women.
- **I040**: Babel is "la tour de lancement d'un astronef prématuré".
- **I045**: Sodom is an atomic explosion, and those who looked became "statue de sel".
- **I180**: miracles are technology misdescribed ("Avions, radio, tout a été transformé en fables").
- **I195, I214**: the same derision of the bearded God on a cloud, and the claim that all mythologies record the same arrival.

Near misses (`similar`):
- **I033/I034**: a nuclear Flood with radioactive fallout, but the ark is a *submarine* carrying documents, not a rocket carrying cells.
- **I041**: Babel's equipment is destroyed.
- **I018**: Eden is placed at Thebes.
- **I196**: Aquarius begins in 1950–54, not 1946.

## Notable Sendy-only readings
- Earth was left "informe et vide" by an earlier human megaton war.
- Humans were not created: they are cave survivors.
- Eve's rib was a mutation operation on the female ("ti" pun), passed down matrilineally.
- Ham saw spacecraft launch documents.
- The rainbow "bow" is an artificial satellite, possibly Lilith.
- Goliath was the last degenerate "Yeti".
- The Ark of the Covenant held Moses' course notebooks.
- The Shema is the law of conservation of energy.
- Tobit was a false prophet who announced Pisces too early.
- Psalm 104 is attributed to Akhenaten.
