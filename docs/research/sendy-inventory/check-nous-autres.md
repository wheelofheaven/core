# Sendy, *Nous autres, gens du Moyen Âge* (1969) vs the TBWTT commentary inventory

**Counts (226 items):** same 3 · similar 19 · mentioned 17 · absent 187.

**What kind of book this is.** It is a short polemic (FeniXX ePub, 7 chapters plus a preface). It sets "pensée médiévale" against "humanisme" and covers Prague 1968, overpopulation, the Templars, Islam and the coming Moon landing. It contains very little verse-by-verse exegesis. The biblical material is mostly Gen 1–3, Gen 6, Gen 9 (the bow) and Moses (Ex 3, Ex 31). For the bulk of the Message's commentary there is nothing to compare: Sodom, Babel, the Exodus miracles, Elijah, Ezekiel, Daniel, Jonah and the Gospel miracles are all missing, so 187 items are absent.

**Strongest `same` items**
- **I006 (Gen 1:2).** The "eaux" are "les nuages opaques dont était enveloppée la Terre". A probe sent to cloud-wrapped Venus "renouvelle" the act of the hovering spirit, so the spirit is in effect a spacecraft over a cloud-covered Earth.
- **I180 (no miracles, only a civilizational gap).** Sendy makes the same argument with a different example. An Amazonian sorcerer shown the Saclay nuclear centre would say "Des êtres à notre image m'ont emmené dans le ciel…".
- **I195 (the bearded God).** Sendy writes of a "néo-Zeus" cult in a world where "il suffit à Dupont de prendre l'avion pour constater l'absence des anges". This is a commonplace idea.

**Notable `similar` items**
- **Serpent (I021).** The serpent is an "Ange Déchu" who told man he was "l'égal des dieux".
- **Expulsion (I025).** Man was expelled for fear he would "jouer avec les manettes".
- **Eden (I018).** Eden is a walled compound, and Adam stands for samples taken from the native population. Sendy says these humans were improved, not created.
- **The Church (I194).** The Church was a popularizer that will collapse "si Elohim désignait Dieu" is disproved.
- **Concessionnaires (I219).** Humans are "concessionnaires", not owners, of the planet. The Message's line is that we are "locataires". The two draw opposite political conclusions.
- **Shared origin myth (I214).** All the first civilizations told one "Mythe commun".
- **End-time signs (I128, I203).** The end-time signs are Jerusalem delivered and the space age. Sendy cites neither Daniel nor Isaiah for this.

**Key divergences.** Several of Sendy's positions contradict the Message:
- Humans evolved naturally and were then improved. They were not made in a laboratory.
- YHWH is an immaterial Principle.
- The Messiah "n'est pas un homme".
- The burning bush was a beacon marking texts that Moses rediscovered. Sendy doubts a live cosmonaut was present.
- Human races and human inequality are cultural.
