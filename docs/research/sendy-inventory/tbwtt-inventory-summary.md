## Most elaborate readings (multi-paragraph)

- **Genesis 1 creation days (2:4–24).**
  A verse-by-verse technological reading: Elohim means "those who came from the sky"; the spirit over the waters is satellites; each day is a ~2,000-year precessional age; explosions built one original continent (with continental drift cited as confirmation); artists designed birds and plants; dinosaurs were monsters.
- **Eden cycle (2:25–45).**
  The tree of knowledge is the scientific books; the serpent is a dissident faction of creators who were exiled; the tree of life is longevity science; the cherubim with the flaming sword are guards with atomic weapons; Israel is the "chosen people" because it won a contest.
- **Flood (2:58–68).**
  Nuclear missiles; Noah's ark is a three-stage rocket carrying one cell pair per species; the argument that the ark rose "above the earth"; radioactivity cleanup.
- **Sodom and Gomorrah (2:78–87, reused at 3:149–156, 5:65, 6:25).**
  Vengeful scientists; a pocket atomic weapon causes the blinding; an atomic bomb falls; Lot's wife is an atomic burn victim. Isaiah 14–15 and 26:20 are read as describing the same blast and its shelters.
- **Exodus sequence (3:7–42).**
  Burning bush (landed craft), pillar of cloud (smoke screen), repulsor ray at the Red Sea, sprayed synthetic manna, water-detector rod, Sinai landing, sapphire alloy floor, "glory" as spacecraft, tabernacle, spacesuits behind "no man can see my face".
- **Samson (3:76–86).**
  The angel fathers Samson while the husband is away; hair and beard are telepathic antennae; Delilah and the temple collapse.
- **The ark of the covenant (3:95–101).**
  A transmitter with an atomic battery: Dagon falls from an electric shock, the Philistines' tumours are radiation burns, Uzzah is electrocuted, and the altar's horns are control levers.
- **Elijah cycle (3:111–133).**
  Electrode-guided ravens, a laser at Carmel, a rocket landing at Horeb, group telepathy, and the chariot of fire as rocket exhaust.
- **Ezekiel (3:157–206). This is the longest single block.**
  Chapter 1 is read as creators in suits with jets; the wheels are saucers and the "eyes" on the rims are portholes; there are service "LEMs" and a mother ship with a throne; the scroll is written on both sides; the dry bones are cloning; the temple of chapters 40–44 is an aseptic base with an airlock, with the "saints/sains" pun and the ban on sweat.
- **Daniel 12 (3:217–227).**
  The last judgement is scientific resurrection; the "time of the end" is the State of Israel plus the space age.
- **Job and Satan (3:248–261).**
  Satan is the leader of the home-planet opposition faction; the "solid clouds" are metal aircraft.
- **Parable of the sower (4:41–63), with the talents (4:78–89).**
  Four attempts at planetary creation, three successful worlds now in competition for the creators' inheritance; the leaven and the mustard seed point to the same three worlds.
- **Walking on water (4:65–72).**
  An anti-gravity beam whose turbulence is the "wind"; the "no miracles, only civilizational gaps" argument.
- **Isaiah 29–65 as Aquarian prophecy against the Church (5:9–34).**
  Zephaniah's Fish Gate marks the passage into Aquarius; the Servant is Raël; the wick is the dying Church; "you are my witnesses" refers to Israel.
- **Kabbalah measurements (5:51–53).**
  Shi'ur Qomah figures in parasangs are converted to light-seconds to give the distance to the creators' planet.

Recurring motifs, useful when searching a 1960s French author: repulsor ray (Red Sea, Jordan), ultrasound "trumpets" (Jericho, Gideon), synthetic food (manna, Elisha's loaves, Jesus' loaves, hosts), telepathy (Samuel, Samson, Pentecost, Psalm 139), the creators' food supply (Leviticus, Gideon, Ezekiel 44), and bases on high mountains (Ezekiel 40, Amos 4:13, Zechariah 4:10 menorah).
