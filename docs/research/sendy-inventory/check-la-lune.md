# Check: Jean Sendy, *La lune, clé de la Bible* (1968)

Source: FeniXX digital edition (2017) of the Julliard 1968 text, `/private/tmp/sendy-study/6.txt`. I read it in full. The book argues a thesis rather than giving a running commentary. Part I covers the Elohim-as-angels translation, the Moon as a "plateforme-escale", Babel, Hebrew and the Kabbalah. Part II covers agriculture, the glaciations and precession. Part III re-reads Genesis 1–2 verse by verse as a colonisation "anticipation". Its biblical coverage is almost entirely Genesis 1–11. It has no Sodom material, no Exodus miracles, no Elijah/Elisha, Ezekiel only in passing, and no Gospels.

## Strongest `same` items

- **I008:** each Genesis "day" is 1/12 of the 25,920-year precession cycle, about 2,160 years.
- **I040:** Babel was a *launch* tower for reaching the heavens. Here the builders are Noah's hybrid heirs.
- **I012:** the fourth day is the Elohim drawing up star maps.
- **I006:** the "spirit hovering" is the spacecraft's radars surveying a cloud-covered Earth.
- **I026:** the cherubim's flaming sword is a manufactured weapon that implies arms factories.
- **I106:** Ezekiel's chariot is a factory-built metal craft.
- **I029:** the sons of Elohim are the visitors themselves, fathering children on human women.
- **I180:** there are no miracles, only a gap between civilisations (the flashlight and cargo-cult examples).
- **I196:** each precessional age is tied to a religion: Aries/Moses, Pisces/Church, Aquarius now. Sendy dates Aquarius to 1950, not 1946.
- **I225:** Hebrew is possibly the language of the "Ange de Iahvé".
- **I005 and I214:** the plural Elohim are visitors "venus du ciel", and the same Celestials appear in all the early myths.

Many `similar` items in Genesis 1 share a frame: Earth under the clouds of the Würm-III glaciation, made habitable again in planned "Tranches". The mechanism differs from the Message, though. Sendy describes cloud dispersal, rain-out and native regrowth. He has no lab creation, no single continent, no artist-designed species, no nuclear Flood and no spaceship ark.

## Notable Sendy-only readings

- **The Moon:** it was deliberately braked into captured rotation as a base. The covenant "arc dans la nue" is on the Moon, which is the "Jérusalem céleste".
- **The Celestials' timeline:** they were based on Mars. Job 38's morning stars are congratulatory telegrams from inhabited worlds.
- **Gen 2:7:** man "from the dust" means glaciation survivors coming out of caves.
- **Eve's rib:** Iahvé teaching obstetrics, which ends the Mother-Goddess cult.
- **Babel:** Mount Meru's height puts Babel's top in lunar orbit.
- **Eliezer:** his name's numerical value is 318 (gematria).
- **Israel:** the name means "de taille à se mesurer avec les Célestes".
- **Iahvé:** compared to Carnot's principle. Sendy also allows Iahvé as the Elohim's king or "président élu".
- **Moses:** a priest of Pharaon who recovered the Pyramid teaching.
- **Precession symbolism:** Ram plus Calf, Pisces plus Lamb.

Sendy explicitly *rejects* a Celestial at the burning bush and accepts natural evolution.
