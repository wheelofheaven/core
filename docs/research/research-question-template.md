# Research question: [short title]

## Exact question

State one answerable question without embedding the preferred conclusion.

## Why it matters

Identify affected claims and decisions.

## Scope

Name texts, languages, periods, traditions, entities, disciplines, and
exclusions.

## Claim kinds involved

List source reports, observations, models, hypotheses, interpretations, or
normative questions that must remain distinct.

## Current basis

List inspected sources and precise locators. State access level.

## Competing answers

Present credible alternatives at comparable strength.

## Discriminating evidence

State what could favour, narrow, or count against each answer.

## Method

Describe search, selection, comparison, extraction, and review procedures.

## Outputs and compatibility

List affected claim records, source notes, public derivatives, datasets,
schemas, and migrations.

## Status and review

Record owner, reviewers, dates, limitations, and unresolved decisions.
