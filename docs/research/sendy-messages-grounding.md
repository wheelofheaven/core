## Load-bearing assertions

- A particular cluster of interpretations overlaps, beyond the generic premise of technologically advanced visitors.
- Evolution and restoration in Sendy differ from the Message's succession of deliberate biological designs.
- Sendy's absent visitors and transmitted inheritance differ from the Message's continuing contact and embassy commission.
- Medievalist reconstruction and reported revelation give shared materials different authority and purpose.
- These differences establish distinct literary projects but do not establish independent origins; selective adaptation remains a serious, unproven hypothesis.

## Research findings requiring editorial care

Raël could have adapted material from Sendy while developing a different cosmology and social programme.
The article should examine the extent and character of possible borrowing.

Points to preserve in the article:

- “Sendy rejects evolution” is too simple: *Ces dieux*, ch. 2, expressly accepts it while challenging explanatory mechanisms and proposing later intervention.
- “Sendy's Elohim no longer exist” goes beyond the inspected evidence: departure and rejection of repeated terrestrial contact are better supported than extinction.
- “The serpent is the same group in both” misses differences within Sendy's own books as well as between Sendy and the Messages.
- “Both teach immortality” obscures different accounts of material continuity and personal reconstruction.
- “The same 25,000-year date” conflates differing numbers and what those numbers measure.
- Sendy's apparent accusation in the 1975 book records his position. The passage supplies no documentation of the alleged reading history.
- The shared treatment of property deserves attention when comparing the works’ philosophical programmes.

## Editorial style

Use direct, concrete prose.
Remove formulaic reversals such as “it is not X, it is Y,” rhetorical questions answered by the next sentence, stock transitions, and repeated declarations of the article’s importance.
Explain substantive differences through passages and examples.
Retain necessary qualifications about evidence and preserve quoted wording.

## Article architecture

1. State the qualified finding and distinguish influence, copying, and religious truth.
2. Establish the chronology and identify the editions actually compared.
3. Present the most specific parallels in a compact table.
4. Develop the differences in biological history, serpent narrative, contact, immortality, and literary authority.
5. Treat Ali's challenge and Sendy's apparent accusation at their evidential levels.
6. Assess direct borrowing, indirect influence, and shared-source explanations; explain what evidence would decide more.

The draft follows this outline and states Wheel's Raëlian starting point while keeping historical judgments independent of that commitment.
Its seven references meet the six-source requirement; these are not seven independent witnesses.
Sendy's books form one authorial corpus, the Messages another, and later critics often reuse both.
