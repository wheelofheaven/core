### B.3 Strength of the identification

**Very strong; effectively certain for the verses checked.** Five quotations from Matthew in book 1 agree word for word with the Grosjean–Léturmy Pléiade text, including three renderings that no other compared version has (« Magnifiques les pauvres par esprit », « Personne ne peut s'asservir à deux seigneurs … détestera », « mal-portants »). The only competitor sharing any distinctive phrase (Osty, in Luke) diverges in the rest of the sentence. Residual caveats: (a) the Pléiade wording was read in a 1980 reprint, with the 1971 first printing confirmed only by search snippets; (b) the Raël wording was read in the 1989 printing, not the 1974 first printing; (c) Osty, BJ and TOB for Matt 6:24 were not read in full; (d) only Matthew was sampled. Book 1 was written in 1973–74, after the Pléiade NT appeared in 1971, so the chronology fits. The book's own bibliography names only the Dhorme Old Testament.

### C.2 What it argues about radiometric dating
