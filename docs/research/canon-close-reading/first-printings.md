## 1. Book 2: prescriptions that were rewritten

The collation located 461 of 473 paragraphs of the later text in B2-1975; the twelve not located fall on pages missing from the scan or are addresses. Most differences are spelling, punctuation, and "Claude Raël" becoming "Raël". The following are changes of substance. All are in "Les Clés" (chapter 3), the part the book presents as written through the author's hand, or in Yahweh's speech in chapter 2.

### 1.1 The hostage rule is reversed

**TEXT.** B2-1975, p. 132: "Devant les prises d'otages penses d'abord à sauver la vie des innocents qui se trouvent entre les mains de ces malades et donnes-leur si tu le peux ce qu'ils demandent. Si tu ne le peux pas, la société doit donner à ceux qui prennent des otages ce qu'ils demandent, car la vie d'un innocent n'a pas de prix et il est facile de rattraper les coupables par la suite, quand plus personne n'est en danger."

Later: QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:158]: ne leur donnes pas ce qu'ils demandent. La société ne doit pas donner à ceux qui prennent des otages ce qu'ils demandent, car accepter un tel chantage c'est encourager d'autres criminels à faire de même et donner du poids à la menace. ENDQUOTE

The 2005 English follows the later wording ("you should not give them what they demand"). The later French also reads "des innocents qui ne se trouvent pas entre les mains de ces malades", where 1975 has "qui se trouvent".

### 1.2 Force against the violent

**TEXT.** B2-1975, p. 131: "Car tu réduiras à l'impuissance les violents par la violence, et au besoin par l'action."

Later: QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:156]: Car tu empécheras les violents de nuire si nécessaire par la force mais toujours sans chercher à tuer ENDQUOTE

### 1.3 An astronomical slip corrected

**TEXT.** B2-1975, p. 139 (printed page number misread by the OCR): "par rapport à la lune qui tourne autour du soleil, et par rapport à la terre qui tourne autour au soleil".

Later: QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:193]: par rapport à la lune qui tourne autour de la Terre, et par rapport à la Terre qui tourne autour du soleil ENDQUOTE

### 1.4 Smaller changes of content

- Meditation posture: 1975 "Assieds-toi en tailleur, si possible sur le sol"; later adds "ou mieux : allonge-toi sur le dos" (ETTMTTP-3:187).
- Judgement after re-creation: 1975 "jugés par ceux qu'ils ont fait souffrir ou par leurs ascendants."; later "ou par leurs ascendants et descendants" (ETTMTTP-2:82).
- Address to Israel: 1975 "un autre pays abritera notre ambassadeur"; later "notre ambassade" (ETTMTTP-2:128). 1975 "vous seriez restés nos ambassadeurs"; later "tu serais resté notre ambassadeur".
- Disposal of the body: 1975 "le plus secrètement possible"; later "le plus discrètement possible" (ETTMTTP-3:69).
- Gatherings: 1975 "sur les lieux de la deuxième rencontre, le Roc Plat, près de Brantôme, en Périgord, à 15 h"; later "en un lieu qui te sera indiqué en écrivant au Mouvement Raëlien de ton pays" (ETTMTTP-3:266).
- A Gospel quotation is rebuilt: 1975 "puisqu'il est le fils de Dieu, qu'il descende tout seul maintenant !" (Matthieu XXVII - 43); later "Qu'il descende (tout seul) de la croix maintenant... car il a dit : Je suis le fils de Dieu" (ETTMTTP-2:111).
- Cross-references to "le Livre qui dit la Vérité" become "la première partie de ce livre"; footnotes are added at "MADECH" and "svastika" (1975 prints "la svatiska").

### 1.5 Not changed

**TEXT.** These read the same in B2-1975 as later, apart from spelling: the correction "que vous avez mal retranscrit" (p. 53, with a footnote to book 1); "il y a vingt cinq mille ans lorsque nous avons débarqué sur la terre" (p. 67); "l'état d'Israël sera détruit une nouvelle fois" (p. 92); the calendar rule "l'an 1946 comme l'an un après Claude Raël … 1976 sera donc l'an 31" (p. 134); punitive re-creation (pp. 79, 85, 88); the passage on the Churches (pp. 150–151).

**OPEN.** The corporal-punishment paragraphs (ETTMTTP-3:16–17) and the sensual-education paragraphs (3:27–35) fall on pages 102–103 and 106–107, which the scan lacks. Nothing can be said about their 1975 wording.

## 2. Book 1: the Bible quotations were corrected and the ellipsis marks added after 1989

**TEXT (measured).** Maridor-D gives 240 entries; 217 could be matched to a paragraph of the later text. Counting ellipsis marks ("(...)", "…") inside the matched quotations: 93 in the transcription of the first printings, 182 in the later text; 84 quotations have more marks later, 21 fewer, 112 the same. The words are identical in 110 entries and differ in 107 (many differences are numerals spelled out, accents, or Maridor's own lead-in words).

**TEXT.** The same passages in B1-1989, read in the OCR:

| Verse | 1974 as transcribed by Maridor | 1989 printing | Later (1998) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exod 14:21 | « Il mit ainsi la mer à sec et les eaux se fendirent » | « li mit ainsi la mer à sec et les eaux se fendirent. » (Exode, XIV-21.) | «(...) il mit ainsi la mer à sec et les eaux se fendirent». |
| Dan 10:13 | « Micaël (sic) est venu là près des rois de Perse » | « Micaël est venu là près des rois de Perse, » | «Michaël (...) est venu (...) là, auprès des rois de Perse». |
| Gen 7:24 | « Les eaux grandirent au-dessus de la terre durant 150 jours » | « Les eaux grandirent au-dessus de la terre durant cent cinquante jours. » | «(...) les eaux grandirent au-dessus de la terre durant cent cinquante jours». |
| 1 Kgs 18:38 | « Le feu de Iahvé tomba, il dévora l'holocauste et le bois et la poussière… » | same, without "les pierres" | «Et le feu de Iahvé tomba, il dévora l'holocauste et le bois, les pierres et la poussière…» |
| 2 Kgs 4:42–44 | « L'homme de Dieu apporta 20 pains d'orge mais son serviteur dit : « comment nourrirais-je cent personnes avec vingt pains ? »… | "nourrirais-je … avec vingt pains" still present | «Un homme (...) apporta à l'homme de Dieu (...) vingt pains d'orge (...). Mais son serviteur dit : «Comment serviraije cela à cent personnes ?»… |
| Gen 1:26 | « …qu'ils aient autorité… » | "ils aient" | «…Qu'il ait autorité…» |
| Matt 5:3 | « Magnifiques les pauvres par esprit » | "par esprit" (per the agent's reading of p. 15 of the scan) | «Magnifiques les pauvres par l'esprit» |

Machine-checked later wording: QUOTE[TBWTT-3:16]: «(...) il mit ainsi la mer à sec et les eaux se fendirent». ENDQUOTE and QUOTE[TBWTT-3:216]: «Michaël (...) est venu (...) là, auprès des rois de Perse». ENDQUOTE

**ANALYSIS.**

1. Two independent witnesses (a 2009 transcription of the 1974 printing and a 1989 printing) agree against the 1998 text. The revision of the quotations therefore falls between the 1989 printing and the 1998 combined volume.
2. The revision moved the quotations toward Dhorme's wording and marked the gaps. In 1974 and 1989 the reader was shown Exodus 14:21 and Daniel 10:13 as continuous quotations; the omitted east wind and the omitted "prince of Persia" clause were not signalled. Lead finding 10 ("ellipses carry several readings") holds for the later text; for the first fifteen years the omissions were unmarked.
3. Not every change goes toward the source: at Matthew 5:3 the Pléiade New Testament and the early printings read "par esprit" and the later text "par l'esprit".
4. In 2 Kings 4:42 the early text had the man of God bring the loaves; the later text restores the verse, in which a man brings them to him. The commentary that follows was not compared.

## 3. Statements about the first printing of book 1 that rest on other people's reading

**TEXT, second hand.** Not seen by me in a 1974 copy.

- The volcano: Maridor (thesis text, section 5.1.2) reports "puy de la Vache" at p. 17 of the first printing and "puy de Lassolas" at p. 13 of the combined edition, and says the second printing (5 November 1974) corrects the photograph caption with a note admitting the error while leaving the body text. B2-1975, p. 33, in the author's own words: "J'avais d'ailleurs appelé à tort ce lieu le Puy de la Vache" (agent report, page image checked by the agent). B1-1989 already reads "Puy-de-Lassolas".
- The movement's name: Maridor quotes p. 141 of the first printing: "Votre mouvement vous l'appellerez le MADECH, le Mouvement pour l'Accueil des Elohim Créateurs de l'Humanité qui porte en ses initiales un message « Moïse a devancé Elie et le Christ »", against the later "Votre mouvement vous l'appellerez le MOUVEMENT RAËLIEN". B1-1989 already has the later sentence and no occurrence of MADECH. B2-1975 uses only MADECH.
- Maridor states that later printings are "revues et corrigées" and that the one correction book 2 asks for was never made in book 1.

**ANALYSIS.** Lead finding 16 predicted that the first printing might differ at "Puy-de-Lassolas" and "Mouvement Raélien"; both are now documented, the first also in the author's own 1975 words. In the 1997 postscript the author writes "Jusqu'à maintenant je n'ai rien ajouté à ces deux textes d'origine" ([paratexts.md](paratexts.md)). Sections 1 and 2 show sentences replaced, a rule reversed and quotations rebuilt; whether that is "adding" is a matter of words, but the texts were not left as first printed.

## 4. The New Testament quotations come from the Pléiade New Testament

**TEXT (measured).** An open scan of *La Bible. Nouveau Testament*, Bibliothèque de la Pléiade (introduction by Jean Grosjean; translated by Jean Grosjean and Michel Léturmy; first published 1971 per the BnF record cited in the agent report) was read as OCR text. Of 48 New Testament quotations of 25 characters or more in the three books, 41 have at least half of their five-word runs in that text (book 1: 36 of 42; book 3: 5 of 7, after removing two book titles the pattern caught). The misses are heavily abridged quotations. "Magnifiques les pauvres par esprit" and "Personne ne peut s'asservir à deux seigneurs" are both there.

**ANALYSIS.** The books name only Dhorme. Dhorme's volumes are the Old Testament; the New Testament quotations follow the companion Pléiade volume. Maridor already notes that the author "ne signale pas que le Nouveau Testament n'a pas été traduit par Dhorme, mais par Grosjean"; the measurement and the name of the second translator are added here. This settles the open point in `hebrew-audit.md` about a second translation for the New Testament, and it bears on woh-claim-0066.

## 5. What the literature already has

The agent's review found one work that does source criticism of books 1 and 2: Maridor's thesis. I checked in its text the passages cited in this note and the following: it states that no academic study of the content existed to the author's knowledge; that book 2 "modifie le premier, sans que celui-ci ne soit retouché dans les éditions suivantes"; that the author marks omissions "de temps à autre par des points de suspension"; that "Claude Raël" becomes "Raël". On the agent's reading (not re-checked by me beyond those lines), the thesis anticipates lead findings 3 (book 2 corrects book 1), part of 5 (paternity), most of 10 (French-dependent readings, irregular ellipses), 12 (who speaks in "Les Clefs") and 16 (edition changes), and does not have findings 1 and 2 (the 2,000-year day and the chronology sums), 4 (Flood motive by book), 6 (proof), the Third Temple and 2035 points, anything on book 3, or passage-level comparison with Sendy. Most English-language scholarship could be seen only as abstracts, so "not in the literature" is provisional.

## 6. Limits

- OCR text, not page images, for both scans; quoted passages were read by me in the OCR and are plain enough, but single words can be OCR errors.
- B2-1975 lacks at least nine pages; B1-1989 is a late printing, so it bounds changes (before or after 1989) without dating them.
- Maridor-D is another scholar's transcription. Agreement with B1-1989 supports it where both are available.
- No printing between 1975 and 1989, and none of book 3 before 2001, was seen. No English edition before 2005 was seen.
- The collation script reports word-level differences after removing accents; it cannot see punctuation other than the ellipsis count, and it filters differences that look like OCR noise.
