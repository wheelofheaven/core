# The canon's scientific and technological statements — register and analysis (key: `science`)

Status: research note for founder review. Nothing here is a decision, a claim record, or an edit to core.

## Exact question

What concrete scientific and technological statements does the canon make — about physics, cosmology, chemistry, biology, genetics, medicine, geology, computing, energy, society-and-technology — and how exactly is each worded?

## Method

- **Texts.** The three French dumps (TBWTT 1974, ETTMTTP 1975, LWTE 1979), every paragraph. I read TBWTT in full myself. ETTMTTP, LWTE ch. 1–2 and LWTE ch. 3–4 were each read in full by a parallel reader under one written sub-brief (`agent-science/SUBBRIEF.md`); I then re-read the paragraphs behind every finding below in the French.
- **Register.** 676 items in `science.json`: REF, voice, domain, specificity (precise / mechanism / vague), framing, one-line claim, verbatim French quote, numbers, note. All 676 quotes pass the verifier (`agent-science/all-quotes.md`); the quotes in this note pass it separately.
- **Voice.** Assigned from context, not from the dump's speaker tag. LWTE is tagged Narrator throughout; ETTMTTP ch. 3 ("Les Clés") is tagged Yahweh throughout but speaks in the human first person ("nos pères les Elohim"), so it carries its own voice label, `rael-inspired-keys`. That label is my reader's judgement, not a ruling (see Limitations).
- **Layers.** (a) what the text says, with QUOTE lines; (b) my analysis; (c) open questions. Outside knowledge is marked `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source]` and is never used to settle anything.

Counts (analysis): 232 items in Yahweh's dictated voice, 161 Raël's commentary, 82 Keys, 45 Raël's narrative, 96 Terrusse, 31 other LWTE contributors, 26 quoted third parties, 3 other figures. By specificity: 111 precise, 343 mechanism, 222 vague. By framing: 187 Elohim technology, 131 nature of the universe, 108 "terrestrial science now", 87 past Elohim acts, 60 arguments against mainstream positions, 57 "terrestrial science will", 8 explicit refusals.

## Findings (ranked; new relative to core)

### 1. The geology and dating apparatus is not in Yahweh's voice; and the three voices place the continental displacement at three different moments

(a) What the text says.

Yahweh, 1973: one continent raised by explosions; the fit of the drifted continents is cited as a recent terrestrial finding.

QUOTE[TBWTT-2:14]: vos savants viennent d’ailleurs de s’apercevoir que tous les continents qui ont dérivé s’emboîtent parfaitement pour n’en former qu’un seul ENDQUOTE

Yahweh's Flood sentence submerges a continent in the singular, and his post-Flood Babel sentence still has one continent:

QUOTE[TBWTT-2:58]: Le continent fut immergé par un immense raz-de-marée qui détruisit toute vie à sa surface ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-2:76]: les dispersèrent sur tout le continent, dans des peuplades primitives ENDQUOTE

Raël, 1979, in his own voice, ties today's residual drift to the *creation* of the continent, 25 millennia ago:

QUOTE[LWTE-1:31]: les effets de «l'orage» de la création par nos pères du continent originel ayant eu le temps de s'estomper, en vingtcinq millénaires ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:31]: au commencement, ils s'éloignaient de plusieurs centaines de kilomètres chaque année ENDQUOTE

Terrusse, signed essay, under the heading "HYPOTHESES", ties it to the Flood strike:

QUOTE[LWTE-4:33]: Nous, Raëliens, avons la clef de cette énigme. ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-4:40]: Lors de l'événement, les plaques continentales se séparèrent brutalement et, dérapant sur leur soubassement de magma visqueux, partirent dans différentes directions ENDQUOTE

Yahweh, 1979 (Apocalypse commentary), describes continents drifting abruptly as an effect of a *future* atomic chain reaction:

QUOTE[LWTE-2:216]: Cette monstrueuse explosion due à la réaction en chaîne fait dériver brutalement les continents, engloutit les îles et balaie les montagnes comme fétu de paille. ENDQUOTE

The critique of dating methods is Raël's commentary and is sourced by him to a terrestrial book:

QUOTE[LWTE-1:28]: Il existe d'ailleurs un ouvrage très intéressant qui développe tout cela très sérieusement pour les scientifiques que cela intéresse, je veux parler de Evolution ou Création ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:28]: l'erreur de ces méthodes de datation est de partir du principe que le comportement atomique actuel a toujours été le même ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. Nowhere in the three books does Yahweh's dictated speech say the Flood broke the continent, mention fossils, coal, oil, strata, or any dating method. Yahweh's 1973 narration keeps "le continent" singular through the Flood and Babel. The breakup-at-the-Flood model, the fossil-fuel ring, the non-recreation of dinosaurs (LWTE-4:54) and the "vitesse résiduelle" (LWTE-4:41) are Terrusse's; the variable-rate drift and the anti-dating argument are Raël's, and Raël's version dates the impulse to the creation, not the Flood. Core `woh-claim-0071` already attributes its mechanism to Terrusse and labels LWTE-1:28–31 as commentary voice; what it does not record is (i) TBWTT-2:76, (ii) that LWTE-1:31 names the creation as the cause, and (iii) that the only Yahweh-voice sentence on abrupt drift (LWTE-2:216) concerns a future war.

(c) Open. Does Yahweh's "continent" at TBWTT-2:76 carry weight, or is it loose usage? Which book is "Evolution ou Création" (the dump gives no author; the promised bibliography is not in the dump)? Null result: chapters 3–4 of LWTE contain no radiometric or carbon-dating commentary at all; the only such passage is LWTE-1:28–29 and LWTE-1:46.

### 2. Recreation carries memory inside the cell; the canon describes no separate memory-transfer step for it

(a)
QUOTE[TBWTT-7:30]: nous recréons complètement le corps tel qu’il était à ce moment-là. Je dis bien tel qu’il était à ce moment-là, c’est-à-dire avec toutes ses connaissances scientifiques et sa personnalité d’alors ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:30]: il est possible de recréer un être mort à partir d'une cellule de cet être car elle contient le plan physique et intellectuel de l'être dont elle fait partie ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:80]: nous pouvons réaliser une réplique totale de l'individu à qui nous avons prélevé cette cellule, avec ses souvenirs, sa personnalité, son caractère ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:33]: Cette matière qui s'est organisée selon le plan qu'ont défini, lors de la conception, les gènes du mâle et de la femelle en formant la première cellule ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:69]: il y a le code génétique qui est la personnalité de chacun ENDQUOTE

Memory transfer *is* described, but as schooling, not as part of recreation:

QUOTE[TBWTT-7:62]: ils subissent des injections de matière cervicale prélevées sur des sujets possédant les informations nécessaires à l’instruction ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. In all three books the cell is said to hold knowledge, memories and character as of the moment of sampling. Two things sit uneasily: the plan is "defined at conception" by the parents' genes (ETTMTTP-2:33) yet holds memories acquired later (ETTMTTP-2:80); and "any cell" (TBWTT-7:30) later becomes a specific forehead cell and a frontal bone holding "le code génétique le plus pur et le plus fidèle" (LWTE-2:158). Core `scientific-immortality` is titled "by cloning and memory transfer" and states "with transfer of memory and personality"; the canon's wording is that memory comes with the cell. The two transfer mechanisms the canon does describe (injection of "matière cervicale", TBWTT-7:62; telepathic waves "s’apparentant à l’électrochoc", TBWTT-4:107) are for instruction.

(c) Open. Is there any passage where a recreated person receives memory by a second operation? I found none; ETTMTTP-2:60 ("stage d'éducation chimique") is orientation, not restoration.

### 3. Three statements are worded as things terrestrial scientists had *just* found — these are the cleanest candidates for dated comparison

(a)
QUOTE[TBWTT-7:62]: Vos savants viennent de découvrir que l’on peut en injectant dans le cerveau d’un rat le liquide de la mémoire d’un rat éduqué, faire en sorte que le rat qui n’a rien appris, sache ce que l’autre savait ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-3:111]: Un exemple de téléguidage des animaux par électrode, comme vous commencez à le découvrir ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-2:14]: vos savants viennent d’ailleurs de s’apercevoir ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. These do not go beyond 1973 science; they lean on it. The rat sentence is the foundation the text gives for Elohim schooling ("grâce à une découverte scientifique importante que vos savants commencent à entrevoir", TBWTT-7:61), and Terrusse repeats the idea in 1979 as an RNA forecast (LWTE-4:132). So one Elohim technology is explicitly anchored to a specific contemporary experimental claim.

(c) To source before any comparison is written: the memory-transfer literature of the 1960s–70s and its later standing `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source: I recall planarian and rodent "memory transfer" experiments (McConnell; Ungar's "scotophobin") that were widely reported around 1970 and later failed replication]`; brain-electrode animal control `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source: Delgado's work, 1960s]`; the acceptance date of continental drift / plate tectonics in French popular science `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source]`.

### 4. The numbers form a largely self-consistent arithmetic, with a few clean breaks

(a) and (b) together; the arithmetic is mine.

- Distance and travel. Parasang = one light-second; 30 million parasangs = 9 × 10^12 km; at 40,000 km/h about 25,700 years ("près de 26 000 ans"); at seven times light speed about 50 days ("moins de deux mois"); 236,000 parasangs = 70.8 × 10^9 km. All four check.

QUOTE[TBWTT-5:52]: Notre planète est à 30 millions de parasanges soit neuf mille milliards de kilomètres environ ou un peu moins d’une année lumière ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-5:53]: à la vitesse de rayons qui sont sept fois plus rapides que la lumière ENDQUOTE

- Chronology. Arrival 25,000 years ago (ETTMTTP-2:43; Yahweh aged 25,000 with 25 bodies, TBWTT-7:56; one body about 1,000 years, TBWTT-7:30); a "day" about 2,000 years (TBWTT-2:10); six days = 12,000; first humans about 13,000 years ago (LWTE-2:208). 25,000 − 12,000 = 13,000: the 1979 figure fits the 1973 ones. Raël adds a Flood about 5,000 years ago (LWTE-1:47).

QUOTE[LWTE-2:208]: Les premiers hommes ayant été créés il y a environ treize mille ans ENDQUOTE

- Demography. Elders know descendants "jusqu’à la cinquantième génération" (TBWTT-7:46): 750–1,200 years over 50 gives 15–24 years per generation, matching childbearing after an operation at 18–21.

Breaks:
- "Depuis vingt-deux mille ans que les créateurs décidèrent" (TBWTT-5:4) against 25,000.
- Lifespan 750–1,200 years (TBWTT-7:30) against "sept-cents ans environ" (ETTMTTP-2:53).
- Trainee eternals: 210 = 70 a year × 3, which fits three *years*; the text says three *existences*.

QUOTE[TBWTT-7:46]: deux cent dix stagiaires (environ soixante-dix par an soit dix par province) ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-7:31]: Durant trois existences, l’éternel est stagiaire ENDQUOTE

- 40 million children among 7 billion: with childhood of 18–21 years and lifespans of 750–1,200 years a steady population would hold roughly 105–195 million children (my estimate, uniform age structure assumed).
- Bread: one pill feeds one man, five times the volume, twenty loaves for a hundred (TBWTT-3:138–139); then "des milliers d’hommes" from a few loaves (TBWTT-4:64).
- Raël's growth analogy shifts its own first-year figure (500 mm, then 60 cm; LWTE-1:29).

### 5. The distance figure was challenged in 1979 and answered by Raël with a variable speed of light; Terrusse answers differently

(a)
QUOTE[LWTE-1:26]: Les scientifiques terrestres actuels disent que la plus proche étoile en dehors de notre soleil est située à environ quatre années-lumière ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:27]: J'y ajouterai que la lumière ne se déplace pas à la même vitesse dans toutes les couches de l'univers, car rien n'est constant ni dans l'espace ni dans le temps ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-4:121]: Toutes les distances en années-lumière calculées par les méthodes traditionnelles sont surévaluées. Les étoiles qui nous entourent sont beaucoup plus près que ce que nous croyons. ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. The book itself records the objection. Three replies coexist: the location is deliberately withheld (Raël, LWTE-1:27); members' curved-light hypothesis (reported by Raël, same paragraph); Raël's own "J'y ajouterai" on variable light speed; and Terrusse's subquantum-medium physics in which stellar distances are overestimated. None is in Yahweh's voice. Yahweh's 1975 speech revises the travel figure, not the distance: seven times light speed "était vrai il y a vingt-cinq mille ans" (ETTMTTP-2:43). The same "rien n'est constant" premise is then reused by Raël against dating methods (LWTE-1:28) and for drift rates (LWTE-1:31) — one premise carries three separate defences.

A further wording point: the home planet orbits at 70.8 billion km from "une grosse étoile" (TBWTT-5:53), is "assez semblable à la Terre" (TBWTT-1:53), yet has days and years of quite different length (TBWTT-2:18). `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source: 70.8 × 10^9 km is roughly 470 times the Earth–Sun distance.]`

### 6. Statements once given as physical facts are later reclassified as staging or pretext

(a)
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:11]: Tout cela n'était destiné qu'à vous tranquilliser en vous apparaissant sous des aspects correspondants à l'image qu'on se fait généralement d'un voyageur de l'espace ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:12]: ils m'ont répondu pour couper court à toute nouvelle demande que cela m'était impossible, comme l'on dit à un enfant que s'il boit de l'alcool il ne grandira plus ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:57]: J'ai utilisé pour rapporter ce que j'avais vu des mots que les hommes actuels peuvent comprendre ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. In 1973 the helmet has a stated mechanism — repulsor rays that pass waves but not air molecules (TBWTT-7:4) — and the atmosphere difference explains Exodus 33:20 (TBWTT-3:42). In 1975 the helmet and flashing lights are "techniques d'approche"; in 1979 the refusal to take Raël aboard is a deterrent told as to a child. Core `woh-claim-0074` lists TBWTT-1:61 and 3:42 as the physiological-divergence layer; the canon's own later reclassification of part of that layer is not noted there. The atmosphere difference itself is not withdrawn (TBWTT-7:68: a residence reproducing Earth's atmosphere).

### 7. Elohim technology is given a history, and its 1973 vocabulary is that of the day

(a)
QUOTE[TBWTT-3:164]: car nous n’avions pas encore trouvé le moyen de voir à travers les parois métalliques en modifiant leurs structures atomiques à volonté ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-3:133]: le feu des tuyères fait que le narrateur parle de chevaux de feu ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-3:166]: Les petites «soucoupes» sont un peu des «Lems» de service ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-7:45]: la surveillance des cerveaux électroniques et des ordinateurs s’occupant des problèmes vitaux ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. The biblical-era Elohim use rockets with nozzles, portholes, metal suits and six small jets; the 1973 Elohim are silent, suitless and see through walls; by 1975 they travel in "quelques instants". The canon is internally explicit that their technique changed over 25,000 years. Period vocabulary in Yahweh's 1973 speech, each `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source]` as a dating judgement: "Lems" (Apollo lunar module), "pile atomique", "cerveaux électroniques", "enfants d’éprouvette", "retombées", "civilisation des loisirs", "coefficient intellectuel", "laser" used as the reference point for four different rays, "cosmonautes" and their "pilules vitaminées", "ce que vous appelez maintenant l’écologie", heart grafts, mouth-to-mouth. The text itself flags "écologie" and continental fit as new words or findings. Statements worded as beyond then-current science: faster-than-light carrier rays and waves (TBWTT-5:53, 5:57), antigravity (TBWTT-4:66), whole-organism recreation from a cell, life from chemicals, lifespan surgery, atom "en circuit fermé" (TBWTT-7:39), moving planets and solar systems (Raël's report, LWTE-1:59).

### 8. Within Yahweh's voice, the two creation sequences differ, and "alone to have created" sits against "many planets created by others"

(a)
QUOTE[TBWTT-5:62]: les poissons, les batraciens, les mammifères, les oiseaux, les primates, et enfin l’homme qui n’est qu’un modèle de singe amélioré ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-2:22]: Après les poissons, ils ont créé les oiseaux ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-5:59]: Mais dans cette partie, notre monde est seul à avoir créé ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-7:23]: beaucoup d’autres planètes sur lesquelles la vie a été créée par d’autres hommes ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. Chapter 2 follows Genesis (plants, sea life, birds with the dinosaurs, land herbivores, carnivores, man). Chapter 5 follows the textbook order of 1973 and puts mammals before birds. The second pair can be reconciled (the other creators have vanished) but the text does not do so. On evolution, Yahweh's wording concedes the sequence and denies only chance: "ne se trompent pas complètement" (TBWTT-5:62). Raël in 1979 goes further in two directions at once: earlier creations and destructions "une infinité" of times (LWTE-1:15) and species that only reproduce the first exemplar's code (LWTE-1:149).

### 9. The infinitely small: "proved" in Yahweh's voice, with three different scale mappings

(a)
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:30]: très certainement aussi évoluée que nous et comparable à ce que nous sommes nous-mêmes ; cela nous avons pu le prouver ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:33]: l'univers étant infini, il ne peut avoir de centre ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:194]: ces galaxies qui tournent elles-mêmes autour du centre de l'univers ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:31]: tandis qu'un millénaire s'écoule, l'être gigantesque dont le soleil est un atome n'a eu que le temps de faire un pas ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. Extends core 0023 and the mass-effect record. (i) The claim is worded as a discovery and a proof, yet the same speech says no communication across levels is possible (ETTMTTP-2:33); how it was proved is not said. (ii) The mapping varies: stars and planets are the atoms (TBWTT-5:57, ETTMTTP-2:31); the whole universe is "une particule d'atome" (ETTMTTP-3:194); beings live on "particules de ces particules" (ETTMTTP-3:195). (iii) The time ratios are given four ways: a millennium per step (ETTMTTP-2:31), "quelques millénaires" per thought, "un milliardième de milliardième de seconde" for the small beings' thought (LWTE-1:44), all human history as "un milliardième de seconde" (LWTE-1:105); the last two are Raël's. (iv) "No centre" (Yahweh) against "centre de l'univers" (Keys). (v) ETTMTTP-2:30 also asserts a weight loss at death and that energy "est, comme la matière, pesante".

### 10. Registers: Yahweh asserts and compares; Raël argues by analogy; Terrusse cites, names equations and hedges

(a)
QUOTE[TBWTT-3:63]: Vous savez qu’une cantatrice à la voix très aiguë peut faire se fendre un verre en cristal ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-1:149]: Une sorte d'alphabet dont chaque lettre serait un atome ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-4:129]: Prenez conscience que l'on a synthétisé des gènes par assemblage de nucléotides; que des segments de molécules d'A.D.N. ont été implantés à l'intérieur de chromosomes bactériens ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-4:61]: Le mouvement des électrons est décrit au moyen de quatre nombres dit nombres quantiques (principal, secondaire, magnétique et de spin) ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[LWTE-4:73]: Rappelez-vous que Hertz n'a découvert les ondes qui portent son nom que dans les années 1920 ENDQUOTE

(b) Analysis. Yahweh's register: flat assertion, household comparisons (bulldozers, soprano and crystal, harbour bubble barriers, radio amateurs), round numbers, no named scientist, no technical term beyond the newspaper level; the words ADN, chromosome, gène, molécule (in a biological sense), mutation, fossile do not occur in his 1973 speech, and "gènes" occurs once in 1975 (ETTMTTP-2:33). Raël's 1979 register: analogy and conditional mood, one book title, unsourced reports ("récemment", "viennent de mettre au point"), a computer–man parallel. Terrusse's register: named authorities as printed (Rostand, "H.-J. Muller, prix Nobel 1946", Planck, "professeur Nehring", "Hudson Institut"), DNA/RNA/nucleotide vocabulary, quantum numbers, an ether-like "milieu subquantique", Einstein's "erreur monumentale" — and at least one slip, the Hertz date `[outside knowledge — unverified, needs source: Hertz's experiments are usually dated to the 1880s]`. The molecular vocabulary of the canon enters with the 1979 contributors, not with the dictation. Terrusse also supplies the only physical mechanism for the "plan cellulaire" transmission (an electromagnetic emission spectrum relayed to a "relais spatial", LWTE-4:75–76); Yahweh's wording gives none.

## Tensions and null results

Tensions not already listed above (REF pairs; analysis):

- Telepathy. Every human brain is a transmitter with hair as antennae (TBWTT-3:85; restated as an "organe télépathique", ETTMTTP-3:185) against Jesus needing Elohim paternity for "certaines facultés télépathiques qui manquent aux hommes" (TBWTT-4:3). Range-limited to Earth's vicinity (TBWTT-3:95).
- Longevity "n’est pas héréditaire" (TBWTT-2:53); telepathic faculty is "par hérédité" (TBWTT-4:3).
- "À notre image", "réplique exacte" (TBWTT-5:64) against a larger human memory volume (TBWTT-7:58).
- "Je ne vous dirai rien" of the planet (TBWTT-1:49) against the distance and orbit given four chapters later (TBWTT-5:52–53).
- Travel "Le temps d’y penser" (TBWTT-1:55), "moins de deux mois" (TBWTT-5:53), "quelques instants" (ETTMTTP-2:43).
- "Vaisseau intergalactique" (TBWTT-7:17) and "niveau intergalactique" for a home under one light-year away; precession attributed to "le mouvement de la galaxie" (TBWTT-5:4).
- Dating: methods rejected in principle (LWTE-1:28), then a 700-year laboratory date used as evidence (LWTE-1:46); old bones accepted as old and assigned to earlier creations (LWTE-1:15).
- Recreation science is far off (LWTE-1:60), synthetic genetic codes "bientôt" (LWTE-1:152), synthetic beings already a marker of the age (LWTE-2:102).
- Robots: "l’un des robots des créateurs, nommé Raphaël" already in 1973 (TBWTT-3:263); biological robots "incapables de souffrance, de sentiments" yet some have pleasure (ETTMTTP-2:53).
- Terrusse internal: Triassic oil ring accepted (LWTE-4:28) against a Flood-era breakup; "au fil des siècles" (4:35) against "milliers d'années" (4:42); "lente transformation en houille et en pétrole" (4:37) inside a frame of a few thousand years.

Null results (looked for, not found):

- No Yahweh-voice statement on fossils, strata, coal, oil, radiometric dating, DNA, chromosomes, mutation or Darwin by name.
- No description of the chemistry by which cells were made "à partir de rien d’autre que des produits chimiques" (TBWTT-2:16); consistent with core 0074's documented silence.
- No named energy source beyond "l’atome" and solar; no propulsion principle beyond "utilisant l’atome" and carrier rays.
- No explicit "vous ne pourriez pas comprendre" formula after 1973; in 1975 the form is deferral ("un moyen technique", ETTMTTP-2:22).
- No "giant computer" in LWTE ch. 3–4; the surveillance device there is a "relais spatial".
- ETTMTTP contains nothing on evolution, fossils, geology or light-years.

## What the canon declines to explain

Explicit refusals (8 items): the planet's identity (TBWTT-1:49; restated by Raël, LWTE-1:27); how "miracles" are done (TBWTT-4:72); the scientific baggage as a whole (TBWTT-5:65); Raël's statement that the Elohim will not demonstrate (LWTE-1:57).

QUOTE[TBWTT-4:72]: vous ne pourriez même pas entrevoir, même vos plus éminents savants, comment nous réaliserions ces «miracles» ENDQUOTE
QUOTE[TBWTT-5:65]: Si nous ne pensons pas encore que nous pouvons vous donner notre bagage scientifique ENDQUOTE

Named but unexplained (mechanism absent): removal of radioactivity "scientifiquement" (TBWTT-2:64); the longevity operation (TBWTT-7:13; ETTMTTP-2:53); the machine that "fournit la matière" (TBWTT-3:184; ETTMTTP-2:70 describes its appearance, not its working); the memory "moyen technique" (ETTMTTP-2:22); remote cell sampling at death (ETTMTTP-2:113); manual transmission of the plan cellulaire (ETTMTTP-2:116); every ray (repulsor, paralysing, refrigerating, antigravitational, sterilising); "ondes de déplacement collectives" (TBWTT-7:47); why nuclear tests are dangerous ("vous ne savez pas à quoi vous vous exposez", TBWTT-7:6); how life on particles was proved (ETTMTTP-2:30). A blanket rule replaces explanation in the Keys:

QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:177]: tout ce que tu ne comprends pas et que tes scientifiques ne peuvent expliquer est dû aux Elohim ENDQUOTE
