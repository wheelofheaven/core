Date: 2026-11-12. Sources read: the held 1998 French volume (*Le Message donné par les extra-terrestres*, © La Fondation Raëlienne 1998), pp. 207–215, extracted with `pdftotext`; the held 2005 English (*Intelligent Design*), foreword and author's postscript; the library text of the three books for the null searches. ## 2. "Third Temple" is not in the three books

**TEXT / NULL RESULT.** Searching the French of all three books for *temple* gives eight occurrences that are not the verb *contempler*. None refers to the embassy.

- Ezekiel's temple as a past residence: QUOTE[TBWTT-3:190]: Les prêtres chargés du service des créateurs dans leur résidence terrestre, le «temple» que visite Ezéchiel ENDQUOTE
- Samson's temple (TBWTT-3:86); Greek temples as house shapes (LWTE-1:179, 1:180); the temple pinnacle in the temptation of Jesus (LWTE-2:38).
- Against temples: QUOTE[ETTMTTP-3:248]: Les Elohim n'ont pas besoin de temple dans chaque ville pour se sentir aimés ENDQUOTE

The phrases *troisième temple*, *premier temple* and *nouveau temple* do not occur. Book 1 does give Solomon's building a name of its own, under the heading "La première résidence pour l'accueil des Elohim" (TBWTT-3:102):

QUOTE[TBWTT-3:103]: Le grand roi Salomon fit construire sur la Terre une résidence somptueuse pour accueillir les créateurs quand ils venaient en visite. ENDQUOTE

The building asked for in 1973 and 1975 is called *résidence* and *ambassade* (the word *ambassade* occurs 2 / 12 / 15 times in books 1 / 2 / 3).

**TEXT.** The identification is printed in the author's postscript of summer 1997 (1998 volume, p. 212): "Le premier temple de la religion juive était en fait, une première ambassade autour de laquelle l'ancienne cité avait été construite. Les Elohim attendent désormais que l'État d'Israël leur concède le statut d'extra-territorialité pour la nouvelle ambassade – le Troisième Temple". The 2005 English postscript has the same sentence ("the new embassy - the third temple"), and Anthony Grey's 2005 foreword says "their first embassy on Earth was Jerusalem's first temple". The 1997 message in the Elohim's voice says *Ambassade* throughout and does not use the word *temple*.

**ANALYSIS.** In the held copies the chain is: book 1 calls Solomon's house the first residence for receiving the Elohim (Yahweh's voice, 1974 text as printed in 1998); the label "Third Temple" for the new embassy is Raël's, in a postscript written 23 years later. woh-claim-0016 records "an explicit identification of the embassy with the Third Temple" as canon-explicit and cites only books 1 and 2. On the held text that wording is supported for "first residence" and not for "Third Temple". Whether the author's postscript counts as canon is a boundary the core has not drawn.

**OPEN.** Earlier uses of "Third Temple" by Raël or the movement (the periodical *Apocalypse*, the 1991–1993 letters to Israeli authorities, *La Méditation sensuelle*, *La Géniocratie*, *Oui au clonage humain*) were not searched; none of those is held.

## 3. The relocation, in two voices

**TEXT.** Book 2 states the condition: QUOTE[ETTMTTP-2:125]: C'est là ta dernière chance, sinon un autre pays accueillera le Guide des guides et édifiera notre ambassade sur son territoire ENDQUOTE

The 1997 message (pp. 207–208, Elohim) says the request now goes "à toutes les nations de la Terre"; that "le rayon de un kilomètre pourra être composé d'eau aussi bien que de terre ferme à condition que la navigation y soit interdite"; that when a country agrees, Israel will have "une dernière fois le choix … durant une période de réflexion très limitée"; and that "Les véritables Juifs de la Terre ne sont plus le peuple d'Israël, mais tous ceux qui nous reconnaissent comme leurs créateurs".

The postscript (p. 213, Raël) is more specific about place: "il est fort probable que nous construirons l'ambassade dans un territoire palestinien ou égyptien ou dans tout autre état limitrophe. En fait, les côtes en bas du Mont Sinaï feraient une excellente alternative".

**ANALYSIS.** Book 2 already said the alternative country "sera proche du tien". The postscript keeps that nearness; the message drops it ("toutes les nations"). The two 1997 texts therefore differ on whether the alternative must be near Israel, and they speak in different voices.

## 4. The return within thirty-eight years

**TEXT.** Postscript, p. 214: "Les Elohim eux-mêmes viendront sur terre dans un futur relativement proche. Dans 38 ans, peut-être même avant, – si la vérité que je décris dans ce livre se répand plus rapidement dans le monde entier". The 2005 English postscript: "It will be no later than thirty years from now and may be much sooner".

**ANALYSIS.** 1997 + 38 and 2005 + 30 both give 2035. **NULL RESULT:** the numeral 2035 does not occur in the three books or in either held volume; the year is only reachable by this sum. The statement is Raël's, in a postscript, and is unconditional in form ("viendront") where the books make the return depend on the embassy (woh-claim-0015).

## 5. Four further statements in the postscript

**TEXT**, pp. 211–215, Raël's voice, 1997.

1. "Jusqu'à maintenant je n'ai rien ajouté à ces deux textes d'origine." The 2005 English calls itself "a newly combined re-translation and updated edition".
2. The symbol: "Déjà en 1990 … ils m'ont exprimé leur accord quant à ma suggestion de modifier le Symbole originel de l'Infini utilisé par les Mouvements Raëliens en Occident". The change is presented as Raël's suggestion, agreed by the Elohim, for the West only.
3. Cloning: "le nombre de clones devra aussi être limité à un par individu... et n'être produit qu'après sa mort."
4. The future religion: "leur propre religion – une religion athée – celle de l'infini".

Self-reported figures, for the record and not checked: 1997 — 25 languages, over a million copies, about 35,000 active members, 84 countries, 130 guides, about 7 million dollars collected for the embassy. 2005 — nearly thirty languages, over two million copies, over 65,000 members, over 200 guides, "seven requests" to Israel.

## 6. One date that does not match the calendar

**TEXT.** Postscript, p. 212: "La première approche a été faite le 8 novembre 1991, le jour du Nouvel An Juif". The 2005 English: "on November 8th, 1991 on the Jewish New Year".

**ANALYSIS.** Converted with the ICU Hebrew calendar (`Intl.DateTimeFormat('en-u-ca-hebrew')`), 8 November 1991 is 1 Kislev 5752, the first day of a month. The New Year, 1 Tishri 5752, was 9 September 1991. Both editions carry the same statement. **OPEN:** whether the approach was made on 8 or 9 September, or on 8 November without being the New Year. The website's "first requested Embassy land near Jerusalem in 1991" is unaffected.

## 7. A loose end from the editions note: the English of books 1 and 2

Separating paragraphs that contain a Bible reference from plain prose: in book 1, 155 of 351 prose paragraphs share at least one eight-word run with the 2005 English; in book 2, 269 of 451. In the paragraphs inspected the shared runs are one to nine per paragraph, against near-total overlap for book 3. **ANALYSIS:** the library's English of books 1 and 2 is not a copy of the 2005 text. Whether it was made with the 2005 text open, or the shared runs are what two literal translations of the same French produce, is not decided by this measure.
