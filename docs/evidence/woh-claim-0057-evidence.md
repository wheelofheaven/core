# Evidence map: The UAP-era reception posture

Serves [`woh-claim-0057`](../framework/uap-reception-posture.md).
`scoped` and `contested`.

## The three grades

| Material | Grade | How held |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documented post-1945 secrecy machinery (FOIA journalism's established record) | Documentary fact | Registered as history; the journalists not enlisted |
| Cover-up-of-exobiology, crop-formation attributions, disclosure claims | Contested claims | Each at its own grade, skeptical accounts in full |
| Any of it as evidence for the framework | **Excluded** | The separation: specific reports (0020) + official conditional return (0015) = no craft in any sky bears on the framework, in either direction |

## The one exception

An official, verified disclosure event is the single UAP-complex
development the separation does not firewall — named in the triggers,
because pretending otherwise would make the firewall the apologetic
architecture the convenient-firewall objection says it is.

## The Dispatch discipline

The PURSUE series and all UAP news coverage operate under this record:
event reported, posture applied, no enlistment either way. Deps 0015,
0020. Milieu-dependence objection preserved.
