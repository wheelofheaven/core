# Evidence map: The early-qibla convergence

Serves [`woh-claim-0024`](../framework/early-qibla-convergence.md).
`scoped` and `contested`. Every layer carries its own grade, and the two
outer layers are forbidden from laundering each other.

| Item | Source | Standing objection |
| --- | --- | --- |

The corpus's Petra reading and its Hijaz-tradition material stand in
deliberate, recorded tension. Resolving it in either direction — in
prose, in either entry-family — is a revision trigger, not an editorial
option.
