# Evidence map: The neo-euhemerist method

Serves [`woh-claim-0064`](../framework/neo-euhemerist-method.md).
`scoped`.

## What the record asserts and what would test it

A method-identity record: its evidence is the corpus's own declared
practice, checkable against the methodology entries and the articles
that apply the method. The inherited components are registered at
their own access levels: Sendy's hologram passage (*L'Ère du
Verseau*, ch. 18) at excerpt access — the French text and translation
carried in the corpus's own entry — and Biglino's three-step
procedure at metadata_only. The corpus's two declared extensions
(precessional architecture; ongoing-alliance frame) distinguish its
method from both inherited programs.

## Controls

Critical: Feder (metadata_only) as the mainstream assessment — the
paleocontact position rejected as pseudoscience, classical
Euhemerism a study-object rather than a live method; the
category-substitution objection (visitors for gods is substitution,
not explanation), which the corpus's own articles state at full
strength; the condition-loading objection (Sendy's equipment
manufactures the coherence it claims to reveal). The hypothesis
(`woh-claim-0001`) is the method's output — kept out of the
dependency list to preserve that order of operations. Open founder
question: `contextual` vs `foundational` relation.
