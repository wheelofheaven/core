# Evidence map: The canon's biochemical layer

Serves [`woh-claim-0074`](../framework/canon-biochemical-layer.md).
Status: `scoped`.

The map weighs one question: does the record state accurately what the
canon says, and fails to say, about the chemistry of the life it
describes? Whether the reported events occurred is `woh-claim-0001`'s
question.

Locators are Wheel `refId`s. Quotations are from the French base text of
the project digitization (access: `project_digitization`). TBWTT and
ETTMTTP English renderings are 2026 machine output and are not quoted.
The provenance of the LWTE English is unverified.

## The silence

The French of all three volumes was searched on 2026-11-08 for this
vocabulary: ADN/A.D.N., code génétique, génétique, gène, nucléotide,
chromosome, protéine, acide aminé, biochimie, silicium, carbone.

| Volume | Hits | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TBWTT | 0 | — |
| ETTMTTP | 1 | « gènes », 2:33 (standard heredity) |
| LWTE | ~25 | Raël's narration; ch. 4 testimonies (Terrusse at 4:129: gene synthesis, DNA transfer). The carbone hit at 1:28 is radiocarbon dating. |

Finding: no passage describes non-standard biochemistry, alternative
genetic chemistries, or the molecular composition of the Elohim or of
other-world life.

Limits of the search:

- It is lexical, and run on the digitization only.
- First printings are uncollated (see `woh-claim-0070`).
- Paraphrastic passages without these words were covered only by the
  2026-11-08 reading of the plurality, atmosphere and synthesis passages
  listed above.

## Source-text observations

- **`LWTE-1:149`, English omission.** The digitization's English drops
  « qui a été fabriqué par les Elohim ».
- **`LWTE-1:149`, letters as atoms.** The gloss's letter-as-atom analogy
  is chemically loose: the letters of the genetic code are nucleotide
  bases. This is mainstream biochemistry, carried as incidental fact.

Both are recorded here and are not corrected in the data-library.

## Dependence

All rows come from one authorial tradition and one channel, so they are
one source in the sense the charter uses, not several.

- LWTE is Raël's commentary on TBWTT and ETTMTTP, not an independent
  witness.
- The Terrusse testimony is a reader's response, not canon voice.

## Controls

The three alternatives in the record, carried at full force:

- `alt-0074-silence-not-denial`: an omission is not a report.
- `alt-0074-image-is-morphological`: only `TBWTT-2:55` bears on genetic
  compatibility.
- `alt-0074-era-biochemistry`: the 1979 gloss restates 1970s popular
  science.
