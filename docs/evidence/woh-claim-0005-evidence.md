# Evidence map: Near Eastern transmission to archaic Greece

This map serves claim
[`woh-claim-0005`](../framework/near-eastern-transmission-to-greece.md). It is
`scoped`. The claim is an `empirical_observation` recording mainstream
scholarship's position; the map's job is to keep the project's *access* to
that scholarship honest, and to mark exactly where the observation's edge is —
because the neighbouring framework claims (`woh-claim-0007`) are only as
disciplined as this boundary.
