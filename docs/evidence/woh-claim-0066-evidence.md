## The identification and its verification state

Marker-verified, not yet collated. The verified instance: the
project digitization carries the distinctive Dhorme orthography in
the canon's quotations — 'Iahvé fit pleuvoir (...) du soufre et du
feu provenant de Iahvé' at TBWTT-WOH 2:86, with the *Iahvé* spelling
(Dhorme's Pléiade rendering of the Tetragrammaton, against the
conventional *Yahvé*/*l'Éternel*) as the marker. The Dhorme volumes
themselves are at metadata_only access; a full quotation-by-quotation
collation against the Pléiade text is the record's named upgrade
path, and until it exists the partial-mediation alternative (memory
quotation, paraphrase, or a second version in places) holds the
identification at corpus-claim rather than per-quotation strength.
