# Evidence map: The translation program's accuracy-first commitment

Serves [`woh-claim-0061`](../framework/translation-accuracy-first.md).
`scoped`.

## What the record asserts and what would test it

A self-descriptive normative record: it states the rule the
translation program binds itself to, and its evidence is the
published apparatus itself — per-verse commentary confining
lens-relevant divergences, glossary records with claim-type
rationales, the editorial/review pipeline with escalation for
speculative entries. Observance is checkable per edition by reading
the translated text against its apparatus; the `direct` label rests
on that checkability, not on any claim that every rendering succeeds.

## The accuracy standard

The accuracy side answers to the text-critical mainstream (Tov,
metadata_only, as the registered standard). The lens side is governed
by the canon's readings — confined, under the rule, to the labelled
commentary layer of every `-woh` edition.

## Controls

Critical: lens-leakage (glossary choices and rendering defaults reach
the text however confined the commentary); self-certification
(a project checking its own rule checks itself — no external audit
has occurred, and the map records none). An external review
documenting leakage into a published text is a named revision trigger
requiring the edition's correction. External translations in the
library are outside the program and outside the record. No deps.
