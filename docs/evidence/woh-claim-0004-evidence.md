# Evidence map: Greek theomachy as conflict memory

This map serves claim
[`woh-claim-0004`](../framework/greek-theomachy-memory.md). It is `scoped` and
`contested`. The claim is a comparative `interpretation`, so every row must
name which relationship it supports — resemblance, typological parallel,
historical dependence, common inheritance, convergence, or referential memory
(see [comparative method](../methodology/comparative-method.md)). No row below
reaches the memory conclusion on its own; the final step is supplied by the
framework in every case, and the map's job is to keep that visible.

Locators: `THEO-WOH` = the in-house *Theogony* translation (line numbering
follows the Greek text; see the
[source note](../../source-notes/theogony-and-works-and-days.md));
`TBWTT` = *The Book Which Tells the Truth*, chapter:paragraph; Odyssey and
Iliad locators are standard book.line references to the Greek text.

## The transmission-fidelity constraint

The claim's carrier is the oral tradition, and the tradition's mechanics are
unusually well studied. This layer constrains the claim from inside its own
evidence base:

| Result | Source | What it establishes | What it means for the claim |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Composition-in-performance: the verbal surface is recomposed every night | Parry–Lord (*The Singer of Tales*, chs. 1–5) | Oral epic is not a memorised text; formulas, type-scenes, and story-shapes are the units of transmission | Structures can survive where wording cannot — necessary for the claim, and equally consistent with every alternative |
| Demonstrated high-fidelity span ≈ four centuries | Boar's-tusk helmet (Il. 10.261–265) against the excavated Mycenaean record; Linear B theonyms (di-we, po-se-da-o) against the Homeric pantheon | Objects, names, and institutional facts crossed the illiterate gap inside the verse | This is the *strongest* verified span. The claim requires structural memory over an order of magnitude longer, per the `woh-claim-0003` chronology |
| The floating gap; schema drift | Assmann (cultural vs. communicative memory); Rubin (constraint systems stabilise form while content drifts) | Oral cultures reorganise the deep past around meaning, deleting the middle distance; events, chronology, causation, and scale are the lossiest layers | The layers the claim needs least (wording, chronology) are the lossiest; the layer it needs most (structure) is the most stable. Nothing forbids the required span; nothing verifies it |

## Mainstream alternatives (all currently at least as economical)

| Alternative | Why it explains the matrix |
| --- | --- |
| Literary transmission | The succession-myth row is *documented* dependence; the combat row has verbal cognates and a shared mountain. Stories demonstrably moved by ship; no referent is required. |
| Indo-European inheritance | Greek also inherits poetic and mythological patrimony from the other direction; the braid (inheritance + borrowing + survival + invention) covers the matrix. |
| Structural convergence (Lévi-Strauss null) | Sky councils, succession anxiety, and knowledge-theft plots are what hierarchical imaginations produce; agreement reflects shared minds, not shared events. Weakest against arbitrary particulars, strongest against shared structures. |
| Social projection | The governing-class texture mirrors Bronze and Iron Age palace politics. Directly targets the claim's most distinctive observation. |
| Category substitution | The technological reading modernises the idiom while preserving the structure (Palmer; Chryssides; Bigliardi). A standing audit on the whole enterprise rather than a rival account of the data. |

## Dependency summary

- The strongest row (succession) supports **historical dependence between
  texts** — the alternative-family reading — exactly as in the
  `woh-claim-0002` map. The pattern repeats: the best-documented comparisons
  argue for routes, not referents.
- Every other row supports **typological parallel** at most, with pockets of
  dependence inside the combat row.
- The transmission-fidelity layer is the claim's most serious internal
  constraint: the verified span is roughly one tenth of the required one.
- No inspected source moves from any of these relationships to **referential
  memory**; that step is supplied by the framework (claims `woh-claim-0001`
  and `woh-claim-0003`), on which this claim explicitly depends.
- A discriminating review would need (a) a criterion separating transformed
  memory from convergence-plus-transmission, and (b) a systematic
  arbitrary-particulars test across contact-free traditions. The matrix
  currently contains one flagged arbitrary particular (the mortal's-help
  rule) and no criterion.
