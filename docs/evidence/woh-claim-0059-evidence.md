`scoped` and `contested`.

## The join's evidence structure

| Layer | Source | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The staged reading (days as working periods) | TBWTT via `woh-claim-0039` | inherited; that record's map controls |
| The lattice (ages, boundaries, anchor) | `woh-claim-0003` | inherited; that record's map controls |

The join adds no evidence of its own — that is the point of carrying
it as a separate record. Its epistemic weight is exactly the product
of its parents', and the `contested` flag registers that the mapping
(which days, which ages, where the week opens) is an editorial
assignment the Genesis text cannot arbitrate.

## Controls

Critical: double-jeopardy (the join inherits every objection to both
parents and adds a suspect equation); schema-convenience (seven onto
twelve requires choices the text cannot decide). Exclusions doing the
disciplinary work: no concordist deep-time mapping; no precision
beyond the lattice's model figure. Deps `woh-claim-0003`,
`woh-claim-0039`; the record moves with the timeline chapters if the
lattice's assignments are revised.
