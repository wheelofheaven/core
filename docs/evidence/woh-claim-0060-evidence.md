# Evidence map: The comparative datasets as tabulated registrations

Serves [`woh-claim-0060`](../framework/datasets-tabulated-registrations.md).
`scoped`.

## What the record asserts and what would test it

A methodological record about the corpus's own instruments: its
evidence is the datasets' published curation rules and the service
mapping to governing claims, both checkable by inspection. The
one-datum rule's exemplar is the documented Atrahasis–Gilgamesh–
Genesis stream (ATRA-WOH; the flood concordance's central column):
however many rows carry that stream, it counts once under
`woh-claim-0010`'s independence principle.

## Controls

Critical: the structuralist account (Lévi-Strauss, metadata_only) as
the standing alternative reading of every tabulated pattern;
curation-selection (tables shaped by the framework's questions);
instrument-drift (publication invites size-reading regardless of the
rule). The forbidden move — counting rows as evidence — is a named
revision trigger. Content-graph and other technical datasets are
infrastructure, outside the record. Deps `woh-claim-0056`.
