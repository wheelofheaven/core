# Evidence map: The Flood as a contested decision

Serves [`woh-claim-0010`](../framework/flood-as-reset.md) 0.2.0. `scoped`
and `contested`. The textual rows summarize the
[procedural comparison](../research/flood-concordance/procedural-comparison.md),
which carries the full cells, editions and qualifications. Cells read as
observations about the named edition and passage; "not stated" is not
proof of absence.

| --- | --- | --- |
