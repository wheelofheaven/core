# Evidence map: Greece within the Elohimian sphere

This map serves claim
[`woh-claim-0007`](../framework/greece-elohimian-sphere.md). It is `scoped`
and `contested`. The claim is a derived `interpretation` whose entire
canonical basis is two short passages in different chapters and different
eras; the map's first job is to display exactly how much weight those two
passages are being asked to bear.

## Canonical layer

| Locator | Era of reference | What it reports | Weight-bearing note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `TBWTT-5:54` | The creator-base era (pre-Homeric in reference) | Greece listed among base regions ("the cordillera of the Andes, the Himalayas, Greece"); its mythology "contains great testimonies" | One clause in an enumeration whose point is that *many* traditions testify. The continuity thesis reads the clause as designating deep engagement; the selective-canon alternative reads it as a passing example. This is the claim's soft spot and is recorded as its first revision trigger |
| `TBWTT-3:214–216` | Classical era (6th–4th c. BCE) | Emissaries among Persians and Greeks for technology transfer (recorded as `woh-claim-0006`) | Chronologically firewalled: cannot support any pre-classical engagement, and is not asked to |

**What the two passages jointly support:** at least two canonically attested
engagements with Greece, different in kind, centuries apart. **What they do
not support:** any narrative of what happened between them. "Continuously
within the sphere" is asserted at the minimum the two moments define; the
map records no third moment.

## Testimony layer

| Item | Status |
| --- | --- |
| The *Theogony* as the systematic Greek testimony 5:54 most plausibly designates | Interpretation; the canon does not name Hesiod. The passage-level readings live in the `woh-claim-0004` map and are not re-argued here |
| Chronological fit | The *Theogony* crystallises pre-emissary (8th–7th c.), which the continuity thesis requires and the `woh-claim-0006` boundary confirms |

## Embedding layer (mainstream substrate)

| Item | Source | Relationship to this claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documented east-to-west literary transmission | `woh-claim-0005` (West pp. 402–403 verified; remainder scoped) | **Consistent with but not evidence for.** The engagement layer predicts the embedding; so does transmission alone. Nothing in the documented substrate discriminates between them — recorded plainly as the transmission-suffices alternative |

## Critical layer

| Objection | Force |
| --- | --- |
| Transmission suffices | The strongest alternative: it explains every catalogued parallel with no engagement layer. The claim currently predicts nothing the alternative does not — this is recorded as a revision trigger, not hidden |
| Selective canon | Two brief passages may not carry a continuity thesis; overreading an enumeration is a known failure mode |
| Category substitution (Palmer; Chryssides; Bigliardi) | Applies with full force to extending the frame over a second tradition's religion |

## Dependency summary

- The canonical layer is explicit but thin; `framework` label, `contested`
  status.
- The claim depends on `woh-claim-0001` (no Elohim, no sphere) and
  `woh-claim-0006` (the second engagement moment and its firewall).
- The claim deliberately takes no support from `woh-claim-0005`: the
  documented embedding is consistent with the engagement layer, not evidence
  for it, and the map says so in its own voice.
- A discriminating criterion — a particular better predicted by shared
  referent than by transmission — remains the open requirement, shared with
  `woh-claim-0002` and `woh-claim-0004`.
