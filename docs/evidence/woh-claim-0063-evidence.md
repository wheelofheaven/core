# Evidence map: The merkavah as craft observation

Serves [`woh-claim-0063`](../framework/merkavah-craft-observation.md).
`scoped` and `contested`.

## Canonical layer (verified)

TBWTT-WOH 3:157-171 (project digitization; heading 'Les soucoupes
volantes d'Ezechiel' at 3:157): the canon's verse-by-verse craft
reading of Ezekiel 1-2 — the quoted vision text, the service-craft
gloss (3:165-166), the frightened observer and the scroll commission
(3:167-171). Primary text at full-text access: Ezekiel 1 (the
inaugural vision); 3:12-15, 8:3, 11:1, 11:22-24, 43:1-5 (the
transports and the kavod's movements the mobile-object reading
depends on).

## Controls

Critical: Greenberg's Anchor Bible commentary (metadata_only) as the
mainstream philological control — the vision as prophetic theophany
built from storm-cloud imagery and Mesopotamian composite-creature
and wheeled-throne iconography; the selective-literalism objection
(the reading keeps the wheels, drops the faces and the enthroned
likeness). Comparative: Blumrich (metadata_only), registered as
independent precedent and cautionary case — his engineering
reconstruction required selective handling of the text, which is why
the record endorses no schematic. Reception: merkavah mysticism and
the Hekhalot literature registered as reception history only. Ezekiel
40-48 excluded to `woh-claim-0016`. No deps.
