## Cross-cultural layer (the extension)

The pattern rows — Titanomachy, Aesir–Vanir, deva–asura, Marduk–Tiamat,
Horus–Set, Quetzalcoatl–Tezcatlipoca, Perun–Veles — are tabulated in the
public Theomachy Cross-References dataset. What the tabulation supports is
recurrence of a combat-of-divine-generations armature; the step from
recurrence to **one remembered referent** is supplied by the framework in
every row, exactly as in the `woh-claim-0004` map for the Greek instance.
Where transmission routes are documented (Greece, the Litan formula),
diffusion explains the resemblance and this claim asserts nothing about
the route.
