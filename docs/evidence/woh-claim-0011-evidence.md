# Evidence map: Monotheism as re-description

Serves [`woh-claim-0011`](../framework/monotheism-redescription.md).
`scoped` and `contested`. The map is a ladder; each rung's epistemic grade
is stated with it.

## Rung 1 — plurality texts (mainstream, direct)

| Passage | What it preserves |
| --- | --- |
| PSA-82 | A council of elohim, judged and sentenced |
| Deuteronomy 32:8–9 | The apportionment of the nations; the benei elohim reading attested at Qumran against the MT's "sons of Israel" |
| 1 Kings 22 | The court scene; the spirit volunteering before the assembled host |

## Rung 2 — monotheism as development (mainstream)

Smith's *The Early History of God* carries the developmental account this
record leans on. Access is `metadata_only`; the wantlist carries it, and
the claim's statement stays within what the developmental account is
publicly known to hold.

## Rung 3 — the functional reading of the polemic (synthesis)

| Tradition | Instance the article assembles | Held at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Israel | Exclusive-allegiance texts coexisting with council poems | project_digitization |
| Paul | "One God" confessed while "many gods and many lords" acknowledged | project texts |
| Qur'an | Rebuke of intercession by people who already acknowledge the creator | project_digitization |

The scope's preservation clause is the control on this rung: the three
traditions' theologies of plurality are irreducibly different, and the
functional reading must survive in each separately or be narrowed.

## Rung 4 — the re-description proposal (framework/speculative)

Canonical anchors: TBWTT-3:4 (worship of stone after forgetting the
creators) and TBWTT-5:21 (the singular-God translation, the hinge —
recorded as `woh-claim-0009`). The proposal currently predicts nothing the
developmental account does not; that parity is a recorded revision
trigger, not a footnote.

## Dependency summary

- Rungs 1–2 are mainstream; rung 3 is honest synthesis; rung 4 is the
  framework layer, labelled `speculative` in agreement with the public
  article.
- Depends on `woh-claim-0001` and `woh-claim-0009`.
- The developmental-suffices alternative is the standing default; the
  claim survives as a labelled proposal, not as an explanation.
