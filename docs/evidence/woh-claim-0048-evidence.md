# Evidence map: Samsara re-read

Serves [`woh-claim-0048`](../framework/samsara-recreation.md). `scoped`
and `contested`.

| Layer | Basis | Grade |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The re-reading at humanity scale: the cycle "not to the individual but to humanity entire"; the demons as aggressiveness; the fall as return to the primitive stage | `ETTMTTP-2:24` | Canon-explicit |
| Awakening re-described: "the state of beatitude through awakening" as the golden age of civilizations | `ETTMTTP-2:24` | Canon-explicit |

Critical layer:
- **Category substitution:** a soteriological structure re-described as
  history.
- **Subject substitution:** individual rebirth moved to humanity's
  trajectory.
- **Frame mismatch:** *anatta* against the separate individual layer.

Dependencies: 0001, 0029, 0031. Karma's accounting is excluded.
Liberation is re-described only as far as `ETTMTTP-2:24` goes.

Open research item: the passage attributes its death-moment account to
"Buddhism" (a vigilant "soul" escaping "demons"). Which Buddhist
presentation that reflects has not been checked against primary sources.
No Buddhist primary text is held in the project library.

Passages inspected for 0.2.0 (French and English columns of the project
digitization): `ETTMTTP-2:22–25`, `ETTMTTP-3:208`, `ETTMTTP-3:214`.
