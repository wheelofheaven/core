# Evidence map: Elohim-civilization hypothesis

This map serves claim [`woh-claim-0001`](../framework/elohim-civilization-hypothesis.md).
It is `scoped` and `contested`. Each row states a passage-level observation and
the competing readings of it; the framework reading is one column among
several, not the map's conclusion.

Locators use Wheel `refId`s: `TBWTT` = *The Book Which Tells the Truth*
(project digitization); `GEN-WOH` = the in-house Genesis translation;
`DEU`/`PSA` = the in-house biblical text. Hebrew is quoted from the inspected
project representation.

which is distinct from whether the report is historical.

**Observation-level summary.** The clustering of genuine plural forms and the
divine-council scenes are real, checkable textual facts acknowledged in
mainstream scholarship (registry anchors: `westermann-genesis-1-11`,
`skinner-genesis-icc`, `cassuto-genesis`,
`deuteronomy-32-8-and-the-sons-of-god-bibliotheca-sacra-158-the-4qdeutj-evidence-`,
`is-isaiah-40-55-really-monotheistic`). The step from "plural and council
language exists" to "the referents were an extraterrestrial biological
civilization" is supplied by the canonical interpretation, not by the Hebrew
data. Recording that step as interpretive is what keeps the claim honest.

## Independence summary

The apparent breadth of the map collapses on inspection:

- Subclaims C and D rest on subclaim A alone; the biblical passages (B) become
  evidence for the hypothesis only *after* the framework identification is
  granted.
- `TBWTT-2:5` interprets Genesis; it is not independent of Genesis.
- Later paleo-contact authors who repeat the proposition depend on the same
  Raëlian and alternative-history stream and are not independent confirmation.
- The genetic-engineering and panspermia sources establish possibility in
  principle and are independent of the canon, but they do not bear on whether
  the specific ancient events occurred.

A reproducible review would therefore need to treat subclaim A's source
criticism as the load-bearing question, not accumulate parallels.
